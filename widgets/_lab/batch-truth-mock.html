§0 · WHY "the noiseless biology" IS NOT ON THE TABLE

One of the two candidate meanings of ground truth was the systematic part alone — gene mean plus disease effect, no measurement noise. Measured, it is not a dataset: every healthy sample becomes identical and so does every diseased one, so the PCA has 2 distinct points, PC1 carries 100% of the variance, PC2 exactly 0, and the separation statistic divides by a pooled sd of zero.

So ground truth = the observed matrix minus the batch shift, which is cell 7's own object. It keeps every bit of biological noise; the only thing removed is the thing the instrument added.
