Widget 39 · the normalize picker as a grid of buttons

Five options — None, Median, Min–max, Z-score, Quantile — in a rail that is minmax(250px, 300px). One row of five gives each segment 45px and Min–max needs 65, which is why the control is a dropdown today. A grid gives each option a whole cell instead. Every group below is core's own .w-seg markup with only the layout changed; the widths under each are measured, not estimated.
