  • On the linear schedule the digit is gone from xt by t ≈ 700, so c107's frames 800 and 999 are both noise.
  • Generation decides the digit between t ≈ 500 and 300 (median 372), the same window in which the forward process erases it, while xt still looks like noise. x̂0 = (xt − √(1−āt) ε̂)/√āt is c96's own formula: the image the noise prediction implies.
  • Predicting the noise is easiest where xt is mostly noise (MSE 0.002 at t = 999) and hardest near the clean image (0.32 at t = 0): the reason c96 samples t at random, “so the model learns all noise levels”.
  • 75 steps (c129) sample as well as 500 (c113): 70% and 61% of samples a clean classifier reads at ≥ 0.9; 10 steps 49%. The 3-epoch model's samples are about two thirds digits.
  • Conditional: the label is read back on 91% of samples (5s 63%); it is learned late, 22% after epoch 1, 59% after 3, 86% after 5.

B: the catalogue's three, Forward · Reverse · Conditional, with Training and Generation on one Reverse page.
Recommend A. Training is where “the network predicts the noise” is drawn (ε beside ε̂, the loss between them), and Generation is where that prediction is used: one page each keeps each page to one press and one picture. Conditional training is the same loop with c concatenated (c124), so Conditional gets Generation only, and its card says so. Stage because the pages are the method's stages; “Step” is the press.

Forward: the t slider and c107's stripA t slider (0–999) drives the equation of images √āt·x₀ + √(1−āt)·ε = xt, the two curves, and c107's six frames (t = 0, 50, 200, 400, 800, 999, click to jump). One ε for every t, so the slider moves the image smoothly (c107 draws a fresh ε per frame). Or no strip.
Recommend as shown. The strip is the notebook's own figure, and with the curves under it the reader sees why its last two frames look alike: √āt is 0.04 and 0.006 there. No press: the forward process has nothing to learn, and the slider is the whole page.

4. Training: what one press and Train showOne iteration of c96's loop on the trained-as-the-notebook model: x₀ → xt (with t and ε) → ε̂ = εθ(xt, t), and MSE(ε, ε̂). Step moves to the next of nine checkpoints (0, 5, 20, 50, 100, 200 batches, epochs 1–3), Train plays them: ε̂ turns from nothing into the noise, the loss curve (every batch, log axes) draws to that checkpoint, and eight samples from that checkpoint's model fill the bottom row. Iteration 1–12 in the rail picks the (x₀, t, ε).
Recommend as shown. Iteration 1 (t = 8) and 4 (t = 930) side by side are the measurement's third bullet in one click: after training, ε̂ at t = 930 is the noise to 0.001, at t = 8 it is still off by 0.17. The sample row is the payoff of Train and the bridge to Generation.

Recommend the rail control: picking an iteration is choosing an example, and a press would put two presses (New iteration, Step) on one page that do different things.
