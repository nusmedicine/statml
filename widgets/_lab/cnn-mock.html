The slot, from the catalogue. 06-1 cell 2's three ideas (the kernel shared across positions, pooling, receptive field growth) and cell 1's parameter argument; 06-2 cell 23 (components and order, calculating dimensions, Flatten against global average pooling) and cell 25's SimpleCNN. The widget trains nothing: every number on this page is arithmetic over shapes.

Where the numbers come from. Shapes run through core's own outSize. A convolution costs in·out·k·k + out and a linear layer in·out + out. The receptive field is r_out = r_in + (k − 1)·jump with jump_out = jump·stride; the square drawn back on the input comes from the backward recursion lo → lo·s − p, hi → hi·s − p + k − 1, which is the same number arrived at from the other end and is what lets the overrun be drawn. Convolutions are stride 1 with padding (k − 1)/2; pooling is k = 2, stride 2.

§1 · The stack, drawn to scale

The same network in three geometries — two blocks, base 32, global average pooling, input 28. A your U-Net figure's convention: flat rectangles left to right, height following H and width following C, the shape printed under each and a 2px rule in the dimension hue under the size that just changed. §2 · The receptive field

The input at the left as a 28 × 28 grid of pixels (a seeded blob and noise, a grey ramp, no literal colours), one unit chosen on a later stage, and the patch of the input it is computed from drawn back on the image. A the patch on the input only. B the patch on every stage between, which is what Step reveals one layer at a time.
