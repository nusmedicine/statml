§2 · Manipulating Dimensions — the verbs in the notebook's three groups

The Operation control becomes three captioned rows, exactly cell 24's list: 1 reshaping reshape · flatten; 2 adding / removing unsqueeze · squeeze; 3 reordering permute · transpose. Two verbs are new. squeeze works on the tensor the notebook squeezes — the one unsqueeze(0) just made, [1, 2, 2, 5] — so its Tensor band shows that source and its Result band the [2, 2, 5] it returns to; a dim that is not size 1 leaves the shape as it is, which is the notebook's own rule and is said in the band. transpose takes two positions, each a dropdown, and its caption says it is permute for two dimensions. §3 · The stack — how far apart the slabs sit

The [2, 5, 2] that permute(0, 2, 1) makes, and the [2, 2, 5] source, three ways. A as built since round 18: exploded, no slab covers any part of another, and the step is 45°, so a five-row slab steps 138px across — the gap Kenneth circled. B the notebook's own drawing: the slabs overlap, the back one offset up and across by one cell at 45°, its top row and right column showing; the cells behind the front slab are hidden, which is why round 3 exploded the stack — a hidden cell cannot light, move or be hovered. C exploded and compact: up by the slab's height and the gap, across by one cell, so the slabs stay close and every cell stays visible; the step is steeper than 45° for a tall slab and flatter for a short one.
