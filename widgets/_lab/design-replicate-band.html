It is static, and the reason is structural: drawSubjects puts a dot at person index × repeat index and never reads a measurement, so every study draws the identical picture. The tile beside it says each study is a fresh set of people and nothing on the figure agrees.

Two things the measurements say before any of this is drawn

1. The obvious pseudoreplication picture is not this model's picture. A subject is N(1, 0.5) and a measurement adds N(0, 0.5), so the within-person spread and the between-person spread are both 0.50 — the intraclass correlation is 0.50 by construction. One person's cloud is exactly as wide as the gap between people, so "tight clusters, far apart" would be a lie. What actually changes with the dial is how well a person's OWN value is pinned:

So the mark that teaches is the person's mean, not the cloud: repeating pins the mean and leaves the people 0.50 apart, which is exactly why the honest test cannot be rescued by repeating. Every candidate below draws that mean.

2. There is real motion available. A person's underlying value moves 0.553 from one study to the next against a between-person SD of 0.50, so every column genuinely relocates each study. The band is frozen today only because neither axis carries a value. And the room is there: at the real width one person gets 66px at 10 people and 22px at 30; at the narrow fingerprint width, 35px and 11.7px.
