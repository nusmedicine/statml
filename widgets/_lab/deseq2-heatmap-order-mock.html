deseq2 · the heatmap's row and column order (his question, round 12)

Is row clustering useful, or would it defeat the purpose of showing the need for transformed values? Clustering reorders rows and columns; it does not change which 30 genes are shown, so the tick count, the transform's worth in one number, is the same under every order below. Measured first (_lab/deseq2-cluster-measure.mjs, 8 seeds): clustering the samples on the 30 most variable genes, 12 seeds at 3 vs 3: the dendrogram's top split is the two groups under counts in 2, under log2(x + 1) in 7, under the vst in 12; the weaker reading the band shows, each group contiguous in the leaf order, holds under counts in 5, log2 in 9, the vst in 12 (6 vs 6: 3 · 8 · 12). So column clustering is a second demonstration on the same figure: the notebook's pheatmap clusters both, and the group band then shows where each sample landed. Seed 3 below (?seed= for another), 3 vs 3, the cell dark to bright on the unit's own scale.

A · as now: rows ranked by SD in the unit, samples in their given order

B · rows by direction: genes higher in B on top, then genes lower in B; samples as given

A sort, not a clustering: within each half the rank by SD stays. Under the vst the two blocks read as the notebook's figure; under counts the halves are still the highest-count genes and no block appears.

C · rows AND samples clustered, average linkage, Euclidean; no dendrogram drawn

The band over the columns is the truth: under counts the samples fall into an order that is not the groups; under the vst the two groups come out as two blocks. Rows cluster into the up and down genes under the vst and into count magnitude under counts.

The call

Recommended: C, both clustered. It is the notebook's own figure, the tick count is untouched, and the column band adds the sample-level lesson the numbers above support: the clustering finds the groups only under the vst. A unit change still slides the rows that stay; the columns slide to their new order the same way. B is the cheap half of it and gains nothing the band does not.

The heatmap's order   C · rows and samples clustered (recommended)  |  B · rows by direction, samples as given  |  rows clustered, samples as given  |  A · as now

The cell's value is the gene's value in that sample: a count, log2(count + 1) or the vst value, never a fold change. The fold change of a row is the gap between its A cells and its B cells, in log2 units under log2 and the vst. The next round labels the cell so on the panel and in the legend.
