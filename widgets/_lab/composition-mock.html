composition · the design picks, drawn to scale

The picks for the composition widget, slot 51: seven pages from PHM5005 05-3, cells 61–101. Ordering, Building, Dimensions, Skip, Gating, Branching, Routing. Every stage is drawn at the real 550 stage width, and the pages that change with the frame at 770 as well, on the geometry the three deep-learning widgets share: PAD 14, GAP 22, usable width 522 at 550 and 742 at 770; layer boxes 30px tall with a 2px border, arrows 2px with a 9px head, shape labels 16px on the edge in mono ink, and the --c-dim-* hue only as a 2px rule under the size that is about to change, never on text. Merge bands are shaded at 12px per cell, one column per feature and one row per batch sample, with the chosen sample's row lit; digits never go inside the wide [4, 20] bands, because at two decimals a cell needs 40px and such a band is 800px.

Colour: the input and branch 1 are --c-group-a, the second operand (a projection, the gate path, branch 2) --c-group-b, Routing's third branch --c-group-c, every result --c-empirical, the thing to look at right now --c-highlight, a torch error string --c-extreme, and Routing's gate column --ink-2. Every random value comes from the seeded makeRng: the batch is randn(4, 10) from one constant seed and every Linear is drawn from uniform(rng, initBound.linear(fanIn)), PyTorch's own default. The code panels are the notebook's forward() verbatim, with the executing line lit.
