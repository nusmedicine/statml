Your question: is shrinkage shown as in 78 (deseq2)? 78's Shrinkage page has two panels: on the left every gene's own estimate against its mean, a trend through them, each gene pulled toward it; on the right one gene's likelihood × prior = posterior, whose peak is the shrunk estimate. For limma that right panel is exact, on the log-SD axis the histogram already uses: the likelihood peaks at the protein's own SD, the prior at s0, and their product is the posterior, which peaks at the moderated SD (s̃² = (d0·s0² + d·s²) / (d0 + d) is the posterior's mode for log σ²).
