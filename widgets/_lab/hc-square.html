Slot 4 · a square data matrix, and what replaces the scatter

It can be 20 × 20, and that fixes the lopsided toggle: 20 objects whichever axis you pick, both with real structure, both giving a 20 × 20 distance matrix. The stage already exists — heatStage({ perBlock: 4, noise: 4 }) is 20 genes × 20 samples with four gene blocks and two sample conditions.

The one cost is the scatter. A point needs two numbers; with a square matrix every object has twenty. So the question is not really the matrix size — it is what goes in the bottom-left panel once a scatter is impossible.

The same table read both ways. Rows are genes with a profile across 20 samples; columns are samples with a profile across 20 genes. Both distance matrices below are 20 × 20 and both have block structure — four blocks one way, two the other.

§2 · WHAT REPLACES THE SCATTER

Three candidates for the bottom-left panel. All show the same twenty objects.

ASK 1. (a) profile plot — each object a line across its measurements, coloured by the cut; (b) nothing, and the tree takes the whole bottom row; (c) keep a 20 × 2 matrix and keep the scatter, as now. (a). It is the picture omics actually uses, it works for either axis, and clusters of similar shape are visible in it — which is what the distances are measuring.
