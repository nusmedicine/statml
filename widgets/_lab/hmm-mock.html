Widget 45 · hmm — three ways to draw the copying model

Same engine, same seed, three figures. Each shows the array-typed sample on top, the reference panel it is imputed from, and the imputed row at the bottom. They differ in what carries the posterior (which haplotype the sample is copying, per site) and whether it is shown at all. Typed so far previews the animation: each step types one more site, left to right, and the whole posterior is recomputed. At 0 the imputation is the panel's allele frequency.
