1 · How a bond shows its mask

Draft: colour alone, both lanes the same width. A: the lane's width carries the mask too (a hairline at 0, thick at 1), and a bond whose larger direction has fallen below 0.5 — one that would not be kept — is drawn as a grey dashed line, so "removed" is a visible state, not an absence; a count under the molecule says how many are kept and removed.

2 · Seeing the masks separate

B: A's molecule, and the left panel gains each bond's mask as a line against the epoch, 39 thin violet lines starting near 0.5 and fanning out to 0 and 1, beside the p(active) curve (a shorter panel above them). The split the optimiser makes, and how slowly, is then one picture; hovering a bond in the widget would pick out its line.
