§4 · The pace — three controls for the same walk

A as built: a slider over Slow (0.9 s a step), Medium (0.34 s) and Fast (0.12 s, results appearing in place with nothing drawn moving). A Join at rank 4 has 80 steps: 72 s at Slow, 27 s at Medium, 10 s at Fast. B two kinds rather than three speeds — Each move (0.34 s, every value drawn travelling) and Results only (0.12 s, values appearing) — because the difference a reader chooses between is what is shown, not how fast; Slow goes, since Step already gives one move at a time for as long as anyone wants. C no pace control: Step and Play, Play at 0.34 s with each move drawn, and a third button, Finish, that places every remaining value at once. C needs one core change (a third drive button); B needs none.
