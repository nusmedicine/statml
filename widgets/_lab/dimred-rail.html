Sections for the t-SNE and UMAP rails

Every widget in the make-data-then-fit family shares the same first block — groups, samples per group, seed, the True-groups reveal — and then the method's own dials. kmeans and dbscan say so with headings (The data / K-Means, settled in widget 23's review rounds 3–4); t-SNE and UMAP do not: t-SNE's perplexity sits inside the data block with nothing marking it, and UMAP's two dials sit above the gates while its seed and reveal sit below the run buttons. Principle 3.4g is the rule both proposals rest on: a control block with two kinds of thing in it says so in its shape.

Every rail below is built by core/controls.js itself from a real parameter spec — same builder, same CSS, same 300 px track — so these are the heights, not an impression of them. The kmeans rail on the left is the shipped one, for reference: it is what "harmonised" would read like. UMAP is drawn at its opening state, so the second gate (Flatten to 2-D) is not yet visible — it appears under the same heading once the graph is built.

Round two: pca and mds — the judgement call

Both already order their rails data-then-method, and their method "block" is the gate(s): a full-width button that draws its own hairline and names the method on its face ("Run PCA", "Measure every pair"). So the open question is whether a heading directly above that button says the name twice, or whether the arc reading identically across all six widgets is worth the repetition. Both B rails are drawn at their opening state — mds's Fit toggle appears under the MDS heading once the gate opens.

Round three: the rest of the collection, under the data/algorithm rule

Kenneth's rule (2026-08-27): where the rail carries a choice of data, it should say so — a data section and an algorithm section. Applying it to the sixteen unsectioned widgets leaves four clean fits and one boundary case, drawn below. The boundary is bootstrap: it has a Population choice, but its "algorithm block" would hold only watch-controls — how many resamples to draw, the reveal, the play speed — because resampling itself has no parameter. Whatever bootstrap gets, clt and permutation-test are the same shape and follow it. em-mixture is drawn with its sample already taken, so the gated half of the rail is visible.
