Widget 104 representation · measured, and the pages proposed

10-1b's four models trained with the notebook's settings (seed 42, Adam 10−3, 10 epochs, batch 128) by _lab/representation-measure.py; the masked model is 10-1b's fixed one (the decoder reads z_vec). Every map is the notebook's own UMAP (15 neighbours, min_dist 0.1, cosine, random_state 42) of the 10,000 test digits, 2,000 of them drawn. "Untrained" is the same network at its seed-42 starting weights. The mock draws finished states so they can be judged; the widget opens empty. On-canvas strings are candidates, not copy. Blue-ruled boxes are my recommendations, grey-ruled what the measurement found, and the plain ones (§8) are the calls.

0 · What the measurement found

1 · The digits are already sorted in the pixels. A 5-nearest-neighbour vote on the raw 784 pixels scores 95.8%, and their UMAP is already clustered (silhouette 0.50). "The embedding sorts the digits" says little on its own; what shows the idea is the same network before and after training.
2 · The autoencoder shows it plainly. Its 16-number code is scrambled before training (UMAP silhouette 0.04) and sorted after (0.57); 5-NN 79.6% → 95.7%. The training signal is the image itself: the untrained reconstruction is a grey blur (MSE 0.21), the trained one the digit (0.011).
3 · The contrastive embedding is sorted before training; the similarity matrix is not. An untrained conv net with a 128-number embedding already clusters digits (silhouette 0.53 → 0.57 after), so its two maps look alike. What training changes is the projection: untrained, every pair of views has cosine similarity 0.999 (all images map to one direction); trained, the two views of one image score 0.96 and different images 0.01. The 10×10 matrix goes from flat to a diagonal, which is what InfoNCE asks for. The gain in the embedding shows as a straight-line classifier: 91.5% → 98.3%.
4 · The sparse autoencoder trades separation for zeros. 57% of its code entries are zero after training (34% at random weights, from the ReLU alone); it separates the digits less well than the plain autoencoder (5-NN 91.7% against 95.7%) and reconstructs worse (MSE 0.021 against 0.009). The notebook's own point for it is the activation histogram, which shows the zeros.
5 · The masked model, fixed, learns as the autoencoder does. 5-NN 93.7% → 96.0%, silhouette 0.44 → 0.56. The training signal is the hidden half of the pixels: untrained, the error on them is 0.24; trained, 0.007.

3 · The four pages, at 550 px

Top: what the model sees, what it must output and the loss, for one digit (here a 3), before and after training. Bottom: the embedding of 2,000 test digits, untrained and trained, in the notebook's UMAP. Digits in §4 A colours here so the maps can be read; §4 is the colour call.

Recommend the strip as drawn, each page's own signal in the notebook's nouns (input, embedding, reconstruction; masked input, prediction; view 1, view 2, similarity). Claims, as measured: after training, digits of one kind sit together in the embedding (Autoencoder, Sparse, Masked); on Contrastive, after training, the two views of one image are similar and different images are not, the map stated as already clustered before training.

4 · Colouring ten digits

The tokens have eight series hues and six cluster roles; MNIST has ten digits, and the notebook colours them with tab10. The trained autoencoder's map, three ways.

Recommend C: the map in ink, the chosen digit in --c-highlight. It needs no new role, reads in both themes, and turns "are the 3s together?" into one look; changing Digit lights another class. A asks for a ten-hue role (two hues beyond the series slots, and several pairs close for colour-blind readers); B is legible only where the points are sparse.

6 · The number under each map

5-NN: share of the 10,000 test digits whose 5 nearest training digits in the embedding are mostly the same digit. Linear: a straight-line classifier (logistic regression) fitted on 10,000 training embeddings, scored on the test digits. Neither is in the notebook; both are the standard checks of an embedding.

A · both under each map (drawn in §3). Contrastive needs Linear: its 5-NN moves 94.9% → 96.2%, its Linear 91.5% → 98.3%.
B · 5-NN only one number a page, read as "neighbours share the digit"; the Contrastive page then rests on its similarity matrix.
C · none the maps alone, as the notebook.

Recommend B: 5-NN is the map's own reading (neighbours) put as a number, and the Contrastive page already has its before-and-after in the similarity matrix. A adds a classifier the notebook does not introduce.
