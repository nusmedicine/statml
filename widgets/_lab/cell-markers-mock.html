cell-markers · Clusters · Markers · Conditions, on the measured stage

Slot 79's six types and four samples carried down to 430 genes of counts, analysed on this page with Seurat's methods as published (_lab/cell-markers-engine.js, the same module the measurement ran): normalise, scale, PCA 20, kNN 20, SNN pruned at 1/15, Louvain at the best of 10 starts, and FindMarkers' tie-corrected Wilcoxon. Each section below is a proposed page. The map places a cell by its six marker-block levels on a hexagon, as slots 79 and 80 do, with a hepatocyte's zonation score as a sideways offset; the clusters are found in the 20-PC graph, not on the map.

1 · Clusters

The resolution sets how many communities Louvain finds in the same graph. Turn Zonation off and move to 0.8: the hepatocytes still split in two, and both halves sit at the same place along the lobule.

2 · Markers

A dot per cluster and gene: its size the share of the cluster's cells that detect the gene, its shade the mean. The table is FindMarkers for one cluster against every other cell, top 12 by p. Grey rows pass p_val_adj < 0.05 and are detected in most other cells too.

3 · Conditions

Kupffer cells only, tumour samples against liver samples, two patients, no condition effect. Each gene is tested twice: over cells (FindMarkers) and over the two patients (summed counts per sample, a paired t). Under no effect a p-value histogram is flat. The sample effect is what one preparation does to every cell in it.
