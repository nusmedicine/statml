A graph constructor for 94's Graph page · mock

Three live frames: the draft as it stands (a press adds the next edge of the four-protein chain), then the same page with the press replaced by construction, clicked two ways. The two prototypes run on core unchanged: every edit — a click in the drawing, a click in A, or typing in the Edge list field — writes one parameter, the edge list, through core's region door, so the URL carries the graph (try Copy link). Nodes 3–6; E and F's features are made up. Nothing here is copy.

0 · The draft: a press an edge

A · Click a node, then another

The first click picks a node (ringed); the second joins the two, or parts them if they are joined; clicking the picked node again lets it go. Directed: the first click is the source. Hover a node for its degree as a row sum of A.

B · Click a pair's dot

A dot on every pair the nodes can form (6 for four nodes, 15 for six); a click toggles that pair, and hovering one previews the edge. Directed: two dots a pair, the one nearer the target. Six slots were searched so that no two dots fall within 15 px.

Recommended: A, click two nodes, with A clickable too. It is how one draws a graph on paper, a directed edge is simply source then target, and it needs no targets beyond the nodes. B's fifteen dots crowd the drawing at six nodes and thirty at six directed, and a dot sits on top of other edges where lines cross. Either way the change is confined to the widget: no core change, one hit-driven fingerprint state.
