Widget 23's rail is twice its figure

Measured on the shipped widget, 1240 px viewport, side layout, real 300 px track: controls 778, drive row 138, rail 951 — against a 446 px stage whose whole column (stage + legend + readout) is 570. The Play button therefore starts 811 px down a column beside a figure that ended at 446, and reaching it scrolls the figure off the top. That is the report, and it is a layout fact rather than a taste.

Every rail below is built by core/controls.js itself from a real parameter spec — same builder, same CSS, same 300 px track — so these are the heights, not an impression of them. Play is ringed in blue. The blue line is where the stage ends (446) and the red line where the figure column ends (570): a rail finishing above red needs no scroll the figure did not already need.

One idea measured and dropped

Shortening the labels to fit the half-width buys nothing. The obvious companion to pairing was cutting “Groups in the data” to “Groups” and “Clusters to look for (K)” to “Clusters (K)”, moving each noun into the detail line as principle 3.4c requires. Built and measured: at 144 px every full-length label already sits on one 18 px line — none of them wrapped — so the shortened rail came out at exactly the same height as the long-labelled one, to the pixel. It is a real cost (a detail is read less than a label) for a saving of zero, so the pairs below keep every label they have.
