His five points on the rebuild (2026-10-18), and the answer to each. The canvas below is drawn at the widget's own width (540 px), so what is small here is small there. loading…

  1. The circle's size changed between pages, it could not move, and on the VAE it covered everything. It was the same circle (95% of N(0, I), radius 2.45) on maps of different scales. Now both models sit side by side on one shared scale (±8), so the circle is the same size on both; codes beyond the view are marked on the edge and counted. The circle is now a sampler: a click or drag on either map moves its centre, and Spread sets its width. At the centre (0, 0) with spread 1 it is N(0, I), the distribution a VAE is trained to match.
  2. Pinning an image had no purpose a student could see. Removed. A click now moves the sampler; the per-image q(z|x) moves into the hover card.
  3. Show it with and without the KL term. The first page is the two models side by side, the same network trained with and without the term, by one Train press. The autoencoder page and the VAE page become one page.
  4. Draw was just digits in a grid. Now Generate decodes the sampler's region as a 6 × 6 grid of points under each map (the circle, decoded), so the grid is visibly the circle's area: without KL the region around (0, 0) decodes to blends of a few digits, with KL to every kind of digit, changing smoothly. Moving the sampler re-decodes it on release. A second mode (a mock-only switch) shows 16 random draws instead, each a dot on the map and a thumbnail.
  5. Hover shows the image dynamically. A card follows the pointer on either map with the decode of the point under it (and, over a dot on the VAE, that image's q(z|x) ellipse and its KL).

1. PagesModel: Without and with KL (the two side by side) · Conditional; or keep three pages (Autoencoder · VAE · Conditional) with the shared scale only.
Recommend two pages, his point 3: one Train press trains both, so every checkpoint compares the two at once.
2. The shared scale±8 on every map (shown; all of the VAE's codes, 75% of the autoencoder's at the end and 35% at 200 batches, the rest marked on the edge and counted); ±25 (every code, but the VAE's cloud and the circle become small); or each map its own scale (round 2, his point 1).
Recommend ±8: the circle is the same size on both maps and the VAE's map keeps room for its digits; the autoencoder running off the edge during Train is itself the point.
3. Generatethe sampler's region decoded on a 6 × 6 grid under each map (shown); or 16 random draws, dots on the map and thumbnails.
Recommend the 6 × 6 grid: its layout is the circle's, so a student reads where in z each image comes from, and moving the sampler shows what each region holds. Random draws stay as Generate's second press if wanted ("Draw 16").
4. The samplera click or drag moves the centre, and Spread 0.5 · 1 · 2 (shown); or the centre fixed at (0, 0) with Spread only.
Recommend movable, his point 1: on the autoencoder a student can find the digits by moving it, and learns that without the KL term nothing says where to sample. The centre is a parameter (in the link); "Reset sampler" returns it to N(0, I).
