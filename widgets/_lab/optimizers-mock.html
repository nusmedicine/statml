§8 · Map and relief

Not a pick, a note. Widget 48's relief renderer is written against a field — projector, reliefMesh and the hidden-line march in widgets/gradients/model.js take any function of two variables — so a display toggle turning this map into a 3D relief costs an import and a call, and the same walk can be lifted onto it with reliefLift. That is what the linked site shows and the one thing it does better than a contour map: a reader who has never seen a level set does not know a pit from a peak. It is deliberately not drawn here, because the picks above are about what the walk does, and a relief decides none of them.
