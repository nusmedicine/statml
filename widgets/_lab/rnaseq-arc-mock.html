1 · Slot 77 count-normalization — counts, CPM, FPKM, TPM and the size factor (01-2 cells 16, 22)

Left, the Composition page's figure: six genes with lengths, one of them (long and abundant) eight times up in sample B, five unchanged. One unchanged gene read in every unit: raw and CPM agree here because the depth is equal; FPKM and TPM read it DOWN because they are shares of a total that gene 5 now dominates; the median of ratios reads it flat because it assumes most genes are unchanged and takes the scale from them. Right, the same at 2,000 genes with 5% up: the log2 shift of every unchanged gene under TPM against the median of ratios, with the 1.5× cutoff.

First to build: it answers ask 1 directly, and it is the widget 39 (normalization) did not build — 39 is scaling against transforming on intensities; this is the UNIT of a count, which only length and composition move. Its last page ends on the size factor, which is where 78 begins.

2 · Slot 78 deseq2 — the negative binomial, dispersion shrinkage, the Wald test, the vst (01-2 cells 1, 22, 24, 35–45)

Left, the Dispersion page: plotDispEsts as the notebook draws it, on 1,200 genes at 3 vs 3 — each gene's own estimate in grey, the trend fitted through all genes as the prior, the shrunk estimate as the posterior. Right, the Transform page: the SD across replicates against the mean, for log2(x + 1) and the vst that the trend implies in closed form; raw counts are off the axis (their SD is the mean's square root and more).

Second, and the heaviest: four pages (Model · Dispersion · Test · Transform). The script's number for the Dispersion page is the arc's strongest: at three replicates the realised false discovery rate at padj < 0.1 is 40% with each gene's own dispersion, 20% shrunk, 8% with the truth. The Test page carries the parked count-regression: the log2 fold change IS the coefficient on the log link, and W = β/SE. LFC shrinkage costs something real and the widget says so: 139 null low-count genes at |LFC| > 1 become 0, and 274 true ones become 194.

3 · Slot 79 cell-qc — nCount, nFeature, mt% and the thresholds (02-2 cells 16–26)

The Thresholds page on the lesson's own cells: the mitochondrial fraction of every cell in each of the four samples, the 10% line the lesson takes from its publication, and how many cells the line removes per sample. The notebook prints one number, 40,564 → 31,014; the script reproduces it exactly and reads where it comes from.

Third. Three sliders, three metrics, and a per-sample count of what each removes. The stage is the call in §7.5: simulated samples shaped like these four, or a subsample of the real three numbers per cell.

For you, from the lesson's own data. The mt% < 10 rule removes 9,351 of HB17 background's 11,197 cells (its median is 14.8%); HB53 background loses 0 of 8,540, the two tumours 1 and 40. CYP3A4 is detected in 91% of HB17 background's cells, so the cells removed are hepatocytes, whose mitochondrial fraction is high in a healthy liver. One patient's normal liver is almost entirely gone before integration.

4 · Slot 80 integration — anchors between batches, and a type that is in one batch only (02-3)

Two batches of cells in 10 PCs, drawn on their first two: three types in both batches, a batch shift, and a fourth type present in batch 2 only (hollow marks are batch 2). Left, before. Middle, mutual nearest neighbours: pairs form only between cells of the same type, so the shared types align and the unique type is moved by the batch vector its neighbours learned and left standing. Right, Harmony: clusters are made to hold both batches, so a batch-only type is pulled toward the nearest shared one — the script: from 8.6 to 3.6 within-type SDs of it.

Fourth. Widget 40 (batch-effect) already says a confounded batch cannot be corrected; this is its single-cell instance, with the mechanism drawn: an anchor is a pair, and a type with no partner has no anchor. The method control is the call in §7.6: each method wins somewhere, which is the rule for offering a choice.

For you. The lesson's layers are patient × type, so tumour against background is integrated as a batch. Its cluster 5 is Kupffer cells by CD163, and 02-4's test of tumor_5 against background_5 returns GPC3 (LFC 9.9, detected in 95% of the tumour side and 0.4% of the background side), AFP, LIN28B, DLK1-like genes — hepatoblastoma cells sitting in the Kupffer cluster. GPC3 is detected in 98% of cells in both tumour samples and 2–4% of the backgrounds. The integration has aligned the tumour with the liver it came from.

5 · Slot 81 cell-markers — what a marker is, and cells are not replicates (02-4 cells 10–22)
