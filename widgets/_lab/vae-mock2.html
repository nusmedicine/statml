Widget 106 vae · round 2: a schematic, training, the KL term, the label, motion and hover

His five points on the draft (2026-10-18), and what this mock does with each. The prototype below is interactive: hover the map, click a dot or an empty spot, press Train, then Draw. loading…

  1. Schematic. The network in the card, in two rows: Training (x → encoder → μ, σ → z → decoder → x̂ → loss) and Generating (z drawn from N(0, I) → decoder → a new image). The Generating row has no encoder, which is the point of the VAE; on Conditional both rows take c.
  2. Training. Train plays ten checkpoints (0, 5, 20, 50, 100, 200 batches; epochs 1, 2, 5, 10) of the same models, retrained with the same seed (_lab/vae-checkpoints.py; the last checkpoint is bit-identical to the model measured). The codes move between checkpoints; a dot runs forward through the schematic and the gradient back, as in 104; the loss curve draws both terms.
  3. The KL term. On the VAE pages the encoder gives each image a distribution q(z|x) = N(μ, σ²), not a point. Hover or click a dot: its q(z|x) is drawn as an ellipse (2σ) in --c-posterior against the circle of N(0, I) in --c-prior, and a panel draws, for each of the two dimensions, the two bell curves and their KL, split into a mean part ½μ² (how far the centre is from 0) and a spread part ½(σ² − 1 − ln σ²) (how far the width is from 1). KL is 0 only when the two curves coincide.
  4. Conditional. Label c as ten buttons in the rail. In the schematic c enters the encoder and the decoder in both rows, and Draw runs the Generating row: each z from N(0, I) and the chosen c go into the decoder.
  5. Tweening and hover. Codes move between checkpoints; on Draw each of 16 points appears on the map inside the circle and flies to its cell, where its decode appears; a hover over the map decodes the point under the pointer, a hover over a dot shows its image, its reconstruction and its q(z|x); a click pins either.
