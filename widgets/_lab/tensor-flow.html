So from my link, choosing U glided the finished result into the new one. Opened plain, the widget starts empty, and choosing U and the dimension leaves it empty until Play or Next step. The options

A · glide only when the argument changes; a different tensor starts empty (recommended). The dimension of cat, stack, squeeze, unsqueeze, flatten, the shape of reshape, the ordering of permute — those rearrange the same values, and a finished figure glides. The tensor itself — U's use, the rank — is different data, and the figure starts empty as invariant 3 says. One line in the widget's `init`.

B · no glide at all. Every change starts empty, as before round 21. Simplest; loses the cat glide you asked for.

C · as built, and my links stop carrying shown=. Nothing changes in the widget; a student who opens it plain sees A's behaviour already, except that changing U after a build glides.
