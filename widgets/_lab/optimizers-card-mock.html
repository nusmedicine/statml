§1 · "rule"

Update rule is standard: textbooks and the PyTorch documentation write "the SGD update rule", "Adam's update rule". "The rule" on its own, as a name for the optimizer, is not — the standard nouns are the optimizer for the method and the update or update step for what it does. Four strings use "rule"; the table says which survive.

Settled, not a pick: update rule for the formula, update step for what is drawn, optimizer for the method; the bare word "rule" goes.

§2 · The card, with the optimizer's descriptor on it

A is the card as built: the name, the update rule, the torch call, and under Adam the AdamW line. B moves the sentence that now sits under the Optimizer control (and under Momentum, and under the Scheduler) onto the card as a descriptor line beneath the rule, in the reading colour rather than the note colour, so the explanation stands beside the equation it explains and the rail says only what is set. Four optimizer states and one scheduler state, A above B.

B. A descriptor under the rule is read with the rule; the same sentence under a segmented control is read before the reader has seen what it describes. It also takes 70–100px out of the rail per state (§3). The scheduler's parameter line moves with it, because step_size 100, gamma 0.1 means nothing without the call it belongs to, which is on the card.

§3 · The rail, as built and trimmed

What is removed and why, in the trimmed rail: the Optimizer detail (self-evident, §1) and its option detail (moved to the card, §2); the Momentum detail and option detail (moved to the card); the Scheduler detail how the learning rate changes over the steps (self-evident) and its option detail (moved to the card); the Map option detail colour alone, seen from straight above (self-evident; Relief keeps its line because it carries the drag instruction); the Learning rate detail shortened. Kept: the Start detail (it carries the drag instruction) and the start's own line, the rate's value notes, Compare's line shortened, the speeds.
