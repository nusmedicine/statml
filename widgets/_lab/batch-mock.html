PHM5003 05 - Introduction to High Throughput Data / 05 - Batch Effect Correction. The misconception: that a batch effect is noise you subtract. The danger is confounding, and correcting a confounded design deletes the biology. Everything below is drawn from _lab/batch-model.js — the notebook's cell 3 plus overlap — and every number in a caption is computed on this page. node widgets/_lab/batch-measure.mjs prints the same values.

§1 · THE CONTROL — how far batch and condition line up

The notebook sits at overlap = 0 and never says so: condition alternates while batch splits at sample 20, so every batch is 10 healthy and 10 diseased. The design, as a 2 × 2, is the thing the reader has to see change.

ASK 1. Does the 2 × 2 count table belong on screen as its own small panel, or is the PCA scatter enough to show the design?

§2 · THE PCA PANEL — two colourings, one set of points

The notebook draws the same scatter twice, coloured by batch and then by condition. That is two panels for one set of points. A keeps it. B uses one panel and a colour-by control. C uses one panel with colour for condition and shape for batch, which is the standard way to put two categorical splits on one scatter — and it removes the colour-role problem rather than working around it, since batch never needs a hue.

ASK 2. A (the notebook's two panels), B (one panel, colour-by control) or C (one panel, colour = condition, shape = batch)?

§3 · THE CORRECTION PICKER

Three options, and the third is the lesson: ComBat(mod = ~condition) is named in cell 11 as "optional but recommended" and cell 12 does not use it. The measured difference is the whole widget.

ASK 3. All three as one picker (A), or a correction on/off toggle plus a "protect the condition" checkbox (B)? B is two controls for what is really three settings, and the middle one is the mistake being taught.

§4 · WHERE THE ESTIMATE GOES

The estimated disease effect against its known truth of 0.80 is what the correction is for, and it is the number that collapses. Three placements.

ASK 4. Tiles (A), a strip drawn under the scatter with the truth marked (B), or both — tiles for the numbers and the strip for the comparison (C)?

§5 · THE END OF THE ARGUMENT — a design nothing can fix

At overlap = 1 batch and condition are the same variable. The design matrix drops to rank 2 and no model can separate them; a fit still returns a number (1.385) and it is an artefact of the ridge, not an estimate. Measured, only overlap 1 is singular — 0.90 still has one sample in each cell.

ASK 5. At overlap 1: blank the estimate and say not estimable (A), or keep the slider from reaching 1 at all (B)? A teaches the boundary; B hides it.
