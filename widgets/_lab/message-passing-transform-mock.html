A · Two presses, ELU on its own curve (recommended)

Aggregate → Multiply by W → Apply ELU. The ELU curve only appears with its press, large enough to read: each number of H·W drops to its place on the x axis, rises to the curve and is read off at the left as h′. The positive numbers come out as they went in; the negative ones (framed) bend toward −1. Measured on these numbers, the bend is small: the four negatives are −0.18 to −0.32 and come out −0.17 to −0.28, because ELU is nearly a straight line close to 0. The curve shows why; the cells alone would hardly show a change.

B · Two presses, no curve: the rule as a gate

The same two presses. ELU is a gate with its rule written on it; H·W passes through it into h′. A positive number passes unchanged; a negative one is framed and its new number appears as it comes out.

Where W comes from

  • W is learned: training adjusts it, together with the classifier, by gradient descent on the loss — the same as any layer's weights.
  • Before training it starts at random values: GCNConv draws it with Glorot initialisation (uniform in ±√(6/(in+out))).
  • The widget's W is that random start, from a fixed seed — nothing trains in the page. Today the label says only "W · one for every node", which does not say either thing.
  • Proposed label: "W · learned in training · here, its random starting values", and one line in the Node control's detail or the page note: "training learns W; these are its random starting values".
