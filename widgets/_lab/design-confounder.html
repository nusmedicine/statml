Widget 44 · saying what the confounder actually does

Nothing on the figure says that a female reads 1.00 higher than a male, or that batch 2 reads higher than batch 1 — the confounder's effect is baked in and invisible. Four ways to fix that, all live on the real engine at 550px, the narrowest canvas the layout allows. Move the confounder dial to see which of them actually tracks it.

What each one costs

A is free and says it in words, but it is a sentence a reader can skip and it cannot show the confounder being absent. B is the only one that lets the reader turn the confounder OFF — and at zero, all three allocations agree, which is the cleanest possible demonstration that the confounder is what the allocation is protecting against. C costs a control and about 90px, and it names the structure rather than the size; it also links straight to widget 26, where fork / pipe / collider are already taught. D costs nothing but a few marks and puts the gap where the measurement is, though it only shows the effect the current draw happens to have.

They are not exclusive: B + D is a dial whose effect you can watch on the axis, and B + C is a dial whose effect you can watch on the arrow into the diagram.
