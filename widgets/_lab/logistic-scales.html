Three things B needed before it could be chosen honestly, all measured on this page and all now applied to every candidate so the four stay comparable:
— tick density follows the panel. Eight labels 64px apart at 486px are the same eight 15px apart at 114px, which the text sweep calls legal and the eye calls a smear. Under 300px the ticks go every ten years.

At 114px the +1 bracket is 3px wide and is not a bracket; the quantity was always the number, and a fixed slot makes B's three panels read as a row of three numbers — +0.91 pp, ×1.0788, +0.0758 — which is the comparison B exists to make.

Widget 15 opens on the probability scale with the raw 0/1 outcomes and no fitted curve. The question this page settles is what the ladder looks like — probability, odds, log-odds — and whether it is a control that swaps one panel or a layout that shows more than one at once.

The claim being drawn: step one covariate by +1 and the shift is a constant on the log-odds scale and is not a constant on the probability scale. Same coefficient, same odds ratio, six times the risk difference across the data. Everything is the notebook's own fit — −6.54292372 + 0.03532089·BMI + 0.07581313·age on the 3658 complete cases.

The raw outcomes exist on the probability panel only. A 0 or a 1 is at −∞ and +∞ once you leave it, which is why the binned proportions are there: they are what survives the change of scale.

Two things drawing it settled that arguing would not have: the odds rung is not straight either — it is a different curve, not a halfway house, so the ladder is curved, curved differently, straight rather than three degrees of the same bend. And with age on x the whole figure, raw strip included, is determined by 39 (age, n, events) triples — 356 bytes, against 39,868 for the 3658 three-column rows. The 40KB inline the catalogue budgeted for is only needed if a covariate other than age goes on the x axis.

What each one costs

Δpx is how tall the +1 year step is drawn, at age 35 and at age 68. It is the whole argument in two numbers: on log-odds it should be the same at both ends, and on probability it should not. A step drawn under 1 px is not a step the reader can see, whatever the caption says.
