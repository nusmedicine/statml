Control styles

Live controls, real tokens. Two questions: what shape should a small ordered choice take, and should “lower panel scale” stay two named views or become a continuum. Drag everything.

1 · Play speed — four ordered options

Speed is a genuine magnitude, so it has a natural order. The wrinkle: the choreography switches off partway along, which is a regime change rather than more of the same, and a control that hides its options hides that too.

2 · Lower panel scale

Currently two named views. A slider could replace them — or replace the whole idea with a continuous zoom. Move the n slider in the last cell: how much zoom range exists at all depends on n, which is the thing to judge.
