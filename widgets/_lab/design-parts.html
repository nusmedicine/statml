Where the three parts go

L1 is drawn at 770px and L2 at 550px, and that is the whole argument. Three panels side by side need 596px of stage; a 550px canvas — the width every fingerprint state is hashed at, and where widget 11's six overflows were found — has 436px. L1 is shown here at the width it needs. L2 puts the same three parts in TIME instead of space and fits anywhere.

Saying what the confounder does

All three replace the four bare decimals. A confounder is two legs — it moves the measurement, and it is unevenly spread across the arms — and the current figure shows the first as two ticks and the second as decimals with "carry it" in 10px grey. Move the confounder dial and the allocation control and watch which of these reacts to which.
