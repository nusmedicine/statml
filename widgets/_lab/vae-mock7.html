What colour by class costs here. Ten digits need ten hues. The palette has eight, and two of them already mean something on these maps: amber is the sampler (--c-prior), violet the highlight and q(z|x). A ten-hue set built from the eight plus brown and grey fails the dataviz validator on the dark surface on every colour check: lightness band (4 hues), chroma floor (brown, grey), colour-blind separation (green ↔ orange ΔE 3.2, against 8), and normal vision (red ↔ orange ΔE 7.1, against a floor of 15). The rule it gives for more than eight categories: never more hues; fold, facet, or focus on one.

C · focus on one digit: a Digit row of ten buttons, and a hover over a numeral

Digit

The chosen digit's codes in --c-highlight on both maps, every other code dimmed, the numerals kept. The same mechanism the Conditional page already uses with its Label, so the reader learns it once. A hover over a numeral on the map previews that digit; a button keeps it. "None" returns to A.

D · small multiples: where each digit lives

Ten small maps per model, each with one digit's codes in ink over the others in grey. Every digit at once, with no hue to tell apart; it costs about 120 px of height a model.

The call

How to show the classesA (as now) · C (focus on one digit, a Digit control plus hover over a numeral) · D (small multiples) · B (ten hues).
Recommend C: it answers the question colour by class would ("where are the 5s, and what do they overlap with?") one digit at a time, keeps colour for the sampler and the highlight, and is the Conditional page's own control. D is the honest all-at-once view if he wants every digit visible together; B fails on this palette and this many classes.
