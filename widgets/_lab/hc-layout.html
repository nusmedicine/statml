Slot 4 · matrix size, panel order, the triangle, and what the matrix does while the tree builds

Four questions from review. Everything is drawn from the shipping model.js; node widgets/_lab/hc-size.mjs prints the size table.

§1 · HOW MANY GENES

Fifty rows over 300px is six pixels a row, and a 50 × 50 distance matrix in a 250px square is five pixels a cell. Everything below still works statistically at every size — the blocks survive a cut into five, the unstructured genes still gather — so this is a legibility choice. One structural point: at 20 genes the gene matrix is 20 × 20, the same as the sample matrix, which removes the size difference the rows-versus-columns control exists to show.

ASK 1. 50 (the notebook), 30 (4 × 6 + 6), or 20 (4 × 4 + 4)? 30. Ten-pixel rows, 8.3px distance cells, the highest rate for the pure-noise box at 91%, and a 30 × 30 that is visibly not the 20 × 20.

§2 · PANEL ORDER, AND THE TRIANGLE

The pipeline is data → dist() → hclust() → cutree(). Reading order should match it. The matrix is symmetric with a zero diagonal, so the upper half repeats the lower half — half the ink says nothing new.

ASK 2. (a) points → matrix → tree, as now; (b) matrix → points → tree; (c) full square; (d) lower triangle only. (a) and (d). The points are the data and should come first; the triangle drops the redundant half and lets the same panel be ~40% larger for the same footprint.

§3 · WHAT THE MATRIX DOES WHILE THE TREE BUILDS

The linkage reads the distance matrix. Which cells it reads is already computed — it is the same witness() the scatter draws its marks from — so the matrix can show the merge being decided rather than sitting still beside it.

ASK 3. (a) matrix static; (b) highlight the cells the linkage read for the merge in flight; (c) (b) plus a box round each cluster formed so far, growing as merges happen. (c). The boxes make the tree's job legible — it is sorting the matrix into blocks — and the cells show which numbers each linkage actually consults, which is the difference between average, complete and Ward stated in the matrix rather than in a caption.

§4 · HOW A DATA MATRIX BECOMES A DISTANCE MATRIX

The step students lose. A 30 × 20 table of numbers is not a distance matrix; you pick an axis, take two of those things, and compare them. Choosing samples takes two columns and produces one cell of a 20 × 20; choosing genes takes two rows and produces one cell of a 30 × 30.

ASK 4. Show this schematic (a) never, (b) permanently above the distance panel, (c) only while the reader is hovering or has just switched the rows/columns control. (b), small. It is the sentence the tab exists to make, and it costs about 60px.
