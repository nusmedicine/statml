What the stage says, measured (arc rates, three seeds). On Finding asserted every strategy is at 88–91% from 16 notes (79% of notes are positive); transfer stops at 95%, the two fine-tunings reach 100% by 256 notes, and training from scratch does as well, because the words alone decide this task. On Drug for the wrong symptom transfer sits at chance at every size: the frozen [CLS] does not hold the pairing (a probe with 4,000 labelled notes reaches 61%). Full fine-tuning reaches 82% and LoRA 78% at 1,024 notes, where the same network from scratch reaches 54%: here pretraining is worth 28 points. So transfer's case is cost (98 numbers trained against 43,874), not accuracy, and fine-tuning's case is a task the pretrained vectors do not already separate.

Measured and left out. (1) The arc's "transfer wins at 16 notes" does not hold with [CLS] (91% against 90%); it was a mean-pooling number. (2) Forgetting: at the lesson's rates no strategy loses masked-word accuracy (80% before and after; LoRA 78% with the adapter on, 80% removed), so there is no forgetting to show. (3) A drug the labels never name (trained on aspirin, tested on nitrate): every strategy is at chance, pretrained or not; this model does not learn that nitrate stands for aspirin. (4) At the lesson's own rate, full fine-tuning is too slow for 400 steps (59% at 1,024 notes on the second task).
