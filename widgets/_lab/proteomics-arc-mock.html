1 · Slot 88 target-decoy — the decoy estimate of the FDR, and peptides to proteins (01-1 cell 2)

5,000 spectra, 60% of them from a peptide that is in the database. Each spectrum's best match is the highest of its correct peptide (if any), the best wrong target and the best decoy; wrong targets and decoys come from one null, which is the whole method. Left: the Score page's histogram, targets above the axis, decoys mirrored below, the threshold where the q-value reaches 1%. Here the targets are split into correct and wrong, which no real search can do (§5.2). Right: walking down the ranked list, the decoy estimate D/T against the true share of wrong targets, and the share wrong among the 200 targets just above each point.

Two pages, Score · Proteins. The Score page's claim measured over 50 seeds: the accepted list's realised FDR is 1.0% (0.3–1.8%), and the decoy estimate tracks the truth all the way down the list; but the last 10% of the list, nearest the threshold, is 3.9% wrong, and the window just above the threshold more. A q-value is a rate for the list, not a confidence in each match. The Proteins page is the parsimony rules 01-1 lists, on a toy a reader can hold in one view; on a simulated proteome (60 families of three isoforms, half present, half of the peptides seen) 89 present proteins become 79 reported groups, 8 of them groups of several, and 11 present proteins sit in no reported group because no peptide of their own was seen.

2 · Slot 89 imputation — where each method puts a value below the detection limit (01-3 cells 27–30, 41–42; 02-3 cells 12–14)

600 proteins at 11 vs 11 (the lesson's design) in log2; the chance a value is missing rises as it falls below a soft detection limit, plus 2% missing completely at random — 34% missing, the lesson's 35.1%. 15% of proteins differ by 1–3 log2; 3% are absent in healthy (six log2 lower). Left: per method, where the imputed values land (filled) against the values that were really there (dashed) and the observed values (outline). Right: one protein absent in healthy — the eleven cancer values measured, the eleven healthy ones imputed. The random forest is missForest as tidyproteomics passes it the matrix: samples as variables, proteins as observations.

One widget, two pages: Missing (where the holes are: the share missing against abundance, the detection limit drawn, the lesson's own decile curve beside it) · Imputing (a Method control over the figures above, and the test each one feeds). Over 5 seeds: kNN and the forest fill from the observed range (bias +1.1 and +0.7 log2), shrink an absence from 6 to 1.8 and 2.6, and kNN's false discovery rate is 26% — its false calls are proteins seen in one or two samples, whose whole profile comes from neighbours found on those one or two values. The minimum and the low draw keep an absence (4.7, 4.1) and the FDR under 5%. Testing only the measured values leaves 123 proteins untestable and finds half the true differences.

Corrected from what I told you in chat. I said a minimum “gives no variance and inflates t”. Measured, the minimum makes FEWER false calls (0.8 of 491), because in a protein with scattered holes it adds low values and so variance. The values with too little spread are the forest's: in the lesson's table, where all eleven cancer values of a protein were imputed, their SD is 0.13 log2 against 0.41 for the measured healthy values.

3 · Slot 90 limma — the variance is shrunk, the fold change is not (01-4 cells 0, 3–9)

2,000 proteins whose true variances follow limma's own prior (d0 = 4, s0 = 0.3 log2), 10% differing by 0.5–1.5 log2. Left: at 3 vs 3, each protein's own SD (sorted) and where the moderated SD puts it — the posterior, pulled toward the prior fitted across all proteins. Right: the same proteins as two volcanoes; the x axis is identical because limma's log2FC is the mean difference, to the last digit. Ringed: the null proteins in the ordinary t's top 50 — small SDs by chance.

Two pages, Variance · Test, with a Replicates control 3 · 5 · 11 per group. The shrinkage matters where replicates are few and vanishes at the lesson's eleven: over 20 seeds, at 3 vs 3 the ordinary t finds 8.8 of 198 true differences and the moderated t 70.3, both at FDR under 5%; at 11 vs 11, 181.8 and 184.7. The refit of the lesson's own table here reproduces its limma t to 1e-10, so the page's arithmetic is limma's.

From the lesson's own tables. On the lesson's data d0 = 3.1 against 20 residual df, so each protein's own variance keeps about 87% of the weight: an ordinary t on log2 calls 678 proteins at adjusted p < 0.05, limma 692. The t-test table's 646 is a Welch test on the unlogged abundances. Cell 9's “smaller due to the shrinkage” is the scale: limma's log2FC is the difference of mean log2 abundances; the t-test table's is computed on another scale.

4 · Slot 91 qc-drift — drift along the injection order, pooled QCs, and what a median cannot follow (02-1 cells 0, 2; 02-3 cell 10)

Simulated, on your call: 26 metabolites with the lesson's names, 40 + 40 samples, a pooled QC first, after every fifth sample and last (17 QCs in 97 injections). Every metabolite drifts by a shared fall plus a slope and a bend of its own. Two metabolites are really higher in B and two lower, by 0.6 log2. Left: one null metabolite in a run made in group order, A then B, before and after a QC-LOESS fit; its drift reads as a difference between the groups. Right: false calls per run over 100 seeds, by run order and correction.

Two pages, Drift · Correction, with Run order Random · Grouped and Correction None · Median · QC-LOESS. Median normalization removes the shared fall and nothing of each metabolite's own drift, so in group order it leaves 15 of 22 null metabolites significant against 16 uncorrected; QC-LOESS leaves 0.6 at span 0.3. Two things the measurement adds: in a random order the median ADDS false calls (1.1 against 0.3), because a median of 26 metabolites moves with whichever real differences sit near it — 77's composition argument, at 26 features; and the QC RSD after a QC fit is read off the points the curve went through, so with a QC every tenth injection span 0.3 reads 0.0% while 0.06 log2 of drift remains in the samples (table below).
