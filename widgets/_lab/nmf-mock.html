The misconception: that NMF is PCA with the minus signs banned — a rotation. It is a decomposition into parts you add up, and its parts are not signed, not orthogonal, not ordered and not unique.

Every picture and every number below is computed on this page from _lab/nmf-model.js. node widgets/_lab/nmf-sim.mjs and nmf-probe.mjs reprint them; node widgets/_lab/nmf-measure.mjs <airway.csv> measures the lesson's own 33469 × 8 matrix.

§4 · THE RANK — where "not unique" actually comes from

The planned claim was "change the seed and W changes", and as stated it is false. Measured on this stage: twelve seeds, rank 2, two real programmes — residual spread 0.00% and every run agreeing to cosine 0.966–0.990. A correctly-specified NMF is reproducible.

What is not reproducible is an NMF asked for more parts than the data holds. Truth fixed at six programmes, six independent stages, five seeds each; the number is the worst agreement between any two of those seeds' parts. nmf-probe.mjs § D runs it wider — eight stages, six seeds, more iterations — and lands in the same place.

And on the lesson's own matrix the instability is already there at rank 2: 33469 × 8 is 2 parts from 8 columns, twelve seeds give a residual flat to 0.65%, parts agreeing only to 0.77, and the treatment separation of the best component swinging 0.68 to 4.21. The figure in cell 23 is one draw.

ASK 4. Rank is then the widget's main control and the seed is a second. Is that the second act — ask for more parts than are there and the answer stops being reproducible — or is it one act too many for a lesson that only ever runs rank = 2?
