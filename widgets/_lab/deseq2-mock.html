deseq2 · four pages, every figure computed here from the widget's engine

His pick at planning: four pages in the NOTEBOOK's order, Model · Dispersion · Transform · Test, on a simulated stage (1,200 genes, a tenth of them changed, three replicates a group unless the control says otherwise). The engine is widgets/deseq2/engine.js; _lab/deseq2-measure.mjs ran it at 2, 3, 4 and 6 replicates first (table at the foot). Blue is a gene's own number (empirical), grey the truth or the Poisson line (reference), red the fitted trend (theory), violet the shrunk estimate or the one thing to look at (highlight). Each canvas is the widget's own width.

1 · Model — one gene's counts, Poisson beside negative binomial; the variance against the mean over all genes

Left: a gene at mean 100 across replicates, and the two distributions with that mean: Poisson (SD 10) and NB with α = 0.05 (SD 24). The six replicate counts are drawn as ticks. Right: every gene's variance across its replicates against its mean, log–log; the grey line is Poisson (variance = mean), the red curve is μ + αμ² at the trend's α. Controls on this page: the mean and α of the one gene.

2 · Dispersion — plotDispEsts: each gene's own estimate, the trend as the prior, the shrunk estimate as the posterior

A: the notebook's own figure at 1,200 genes — gene-wise (blue), trend (red), MAP (violet), the outliers left on their own estimate ringed. B: the exposition's figure (deseq2-shrinkage.png) — the same, on 60 genes, with an arrow from each gene's own estimate to its shrunk one, so the pull toward the trend is drawn. The readout under either: null genes called at padj < 0.1 with the gene-wise and with the shrunk dispersion, which is the empirical Bayes argument in one number. Control: replicates 2 · 3 · 4 · 6.

3 · Transform — the SD across replicates against the mean: raw counts, log2(x + 1), the vst

Left: one dot per null gene, the SD of its normalised counts across the six replicates against its mean, under each transform (raw counts are off the axis: their SD is the square root of the mean and more). Right: the two transforms as curves against the count, where the vst leaves log2 near zero and joins it above a hundred. This is why the PCA and the heatmap take vsd.

4 · Test — one gene's fit, and the MA plot over all genes, before and after LFC shrinkage

Left: one changed gene — its replicates in each group on a log2 axis after the size factors, the two group means (the GLM's two coefficients on the log link), β as their difference, the SE bar from the working weights, W and p. Middle and right: the MA plot as plotMA draws it, padj < 0.1 coloured; then after LFC shrinkage, with what it removed and what it cost printed.
