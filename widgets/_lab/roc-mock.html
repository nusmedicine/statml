§2 · The entry — the curve is TRACED by the sweeping threshold

No widget opens on its answer, and the ROC curve is the answer here. The entry animation: the threshold line sweeps down the strip from 1 to 0, and every patient it passes bends the path — up for a death, right for a survivor. The curve is built one patient at a time, which is also what AUC means (a random death ranking above a random survivor). Mid-sweep frame below: threshold at 0.45, the path ahead ghosted.
