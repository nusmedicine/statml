0 · What the measurement found

1 · Page 1, Catalogue: one tumour's mutations into 96 types (cells 3–18)

One simulated tumour with 1,302 single-base substitutions, as many as cell 14's TCGA-BH-A18G. A MAF writes each change on the reference strand, so half of them arrive as a change from a purine (G or A) — 41,398 of the lesson's 82,747 do. One press each: the changes as written; each read from the pyrimidine of its base pair, which folds twelve changes into six (cells 3–6's transitions and transversions are read here); and each split by the base on either side, which makes 96 (cells 10–18).

2 · Page 2, Signatures: the cohort factorised (cells 0 and 19–21)

Four processes are planted: deamination at CpG and a flat clock-like process in every tumour, APOBEC (its C>T and its C>G together, as the enzymes make them) in about a third, and a homologous recombination defect in about a quarter. One tumour is hypermutated: a polymerase epsilon profile carrying 7.1% of the cohort's mutations, TCGA-AN-A046's share of the lesson's. Four signatures are extracted, the number planted, with pConstant = 0.1 as cell 21 has it. A and B draw the same extraction.

3 · How large must one tumour be to take a signature?

The same cohort shape, the hypermutated tumour's share of all mutations varied, four signatures extracted from one start, seeds 1–5. The measure script runs ten seeds (§6 of its output): 0 of 10 at 1%, 6 of 10 at 3%, 9 of 10 at 7.1%. A start that misses it stops at a higher divergence: the tumour's own signature is the better fit.

4 · Page 3, Matching: naming each signature (cells 22–34)

The four signatures of §2 against the ten references of §5 by cosine similarity, which is what compareSignatures computes. Because the cohort is simulated, the page also knows what built each signature: the non-negative mix of the planted profiles closest to it, printed under each. A is cells 26 and 32's heatmap; B is cells 28 and 34's profiles, each with the runner-up beside the best match.

5 · The references: ten look-alikes, not COSMIC's profiles

COSMIC's terms forbid exposing its data “on a free to access platform (including but not limited to public facing websites)” without GRL's written consent (cosmickb.org/terms, read 2026-09-18), and the gallery is one. Each profile below is built for this page from its process's mechanism — which base changes, beside which neighbours — and not from COSMIC's numbers. The measure scores each against the real profile it stands for on this machine (maftools ships them); those cosines are printed and no vector leaves the repo. The catalogue's geometry follows the real one: the flat three sit 0.78–0.87 apart (real 0.79–0.88), CpG against mismatch repair 0.81 (real 0.77).

8.1 · The six substitution classes' colours

Your two figures disagree: cell 18 draws ggplot's default hues and cells 28 and 34 draw maftools' (coral4, lightcyan4, deeppink3, lightsalmon1, forestgreen, cornflowerblue, read from the installed source). The field's own convention is the one COSMIC's site and SigProfilerPlotting draw: C>A light blue, C>G black, C>T red, T>A light grey, T>C light green, T>G pale pink (read from SigProfilerPlotting's source). Whichever is picked becomes six roles in tokens.css, a core change with a full fingerprint run. Each arm draws the same tumour.
