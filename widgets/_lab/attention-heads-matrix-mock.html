In torch nothing runs one query at a time: every head's weights are an [L × L] matrix, every head's output is Zₕ = Aₕ Vₕ, an [L × 12] matrix, the four sit side by side as [L × 48], and × W_Oᵀ + b gives [L × 48], all in one pass. The rows do not depend on each other, so filling them a query at a time gives the same numbers.
