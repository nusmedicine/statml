cell-markers · the four comparisons, on the widget's stage

Every table and figure here is computed on this page from widgets/cell-markers/engine.js and widget 78's DESeq2 engine, the modules the measurement ran (_lab/cell-markers-compare-measure.txt). For clarity the mock groups cells by their TRUE type; the widget would use the clusters found on its Clusters page.

1 · Cluster against cluster — the comparison decides what a marker is

Tumour cells tested twice with FindMarkers, first 14 up by p. Highlighted rows are the 10 genes tumour cells share with hepatocytes (hepatoblastoma is a tumour of hepatocyte precursors). Against all other cells some of them read as tumour markers; against hepatocytes they are lower in tumour cells, and leave the list.

2 · Conserved markers — a marker true in each patient, not only in the pool

Hepatocytes against all other cells. Highlighted rows are 10 genes raised in patient 1's hepatocytes only. Pooled, they reach p_val_adj < 0.05; tested within each patient and combined (FindConservedMarkers, metap's minimump), they do not.

3 · One cell type across conditions — identifying genes

Kupffer cells, tumour against liver, 20 genes truly changed (ringed). Each dot a gene: its fold change and how small its p-value is, over cells (Wilcoxon) and over the four samples (DESeq2 on summed counts). Filled: called at adjusted p < 0.05.

4 · Composition — does a cell type's share change?

Each sample's share of each cell type, and a test per type of liver against tumour: over cells (the pooled counts, a chi-square) and over samples (the four shares, arcsine square root, a t-test on 2 against 2). Each sample's mix varies around its tissue's.
