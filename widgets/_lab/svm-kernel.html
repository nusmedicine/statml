Showing the kernel transformation

The three asks pull in one direction, and there is a cost worth naming before you look. Making the kernel the subject means the data has to be generated, because none of PHM5005's own data sets can carry it — measured below. So this would be the first widget in the PHM5005 arc that does not stand on a course notebook’s data. Widgets 1–11 all generate from a seed, so it is not new to the collection; it is new to this arc.

The alternative is to answer 1 and 3 and drop 2: keep the margin as the subject, keep the colorectal biopsies, cut the second data set and the hinge loss, and let the plane have the full width. That is the smaller widget and it keeps real data.

Below is what the kernel version looks like.

Two panels, and the same fit in both. On the left, the measurements as they were taken. On the right, the same samples after the feature map — where the boundary is a straight line with a corridor. Switch the kernel and watch the left-hand boundary bend while the right-hand one stays straight. That is the whole idea: the SVM only ever draws a straight line, and the kernel decides which space it draws it in.

The map is φ(z1, z2) = (z1², z2²) — a real member of the degree-2 polynomial family, and one that lands in two dimensions rather than six, so the lifted space can be drawn rather than described. A boundary at z1² + z2² = c is a circle on the left and the line u + v = c on the right, exactly.

The data is generated, and it has to be. None of PHM5005's three data sets can carry this — measured, not assumed:

datawhat a kernel adds
Colorectal biopsies (03-5) nothing. 2,258 probes have |AUC − 0.5| > 0.45 and one separates the classes alone; of 435 mid-strength pairs, 0 need a curve
Heart failure (04-3) 2.4–4.0 CV points on the best 2-feature pairs — real but invisible — and on all features RBF is worse than linear, 0.729 against 0.736. Ejection fraction is not U-shaped: the 50–60 uptick is 7 deaths in 24 patients and 60+ drops back
Body fat (04-1/2/3) regression, not classification

So the samples below are drawn from a seeded generator, like widgets 1–11. The clinical frame is real, though, and it is the reason the boundary is a ring: a reference range has two ends. Sodium under 135 is abnormal and over 145 is abnormal; the same for potassium either side of 3.5–5.0. “Normal” is a region in the middle, and no threshold — no straight line — can carve a middle out.

Same figure, kernel = straight line forced, so the two can be compared without reaching for the control. The map is the identity, so the two panels are the same panel — which is the honest way to say that a linear SVM does no transformation at all. The boundary cuts the ring in half and misclassifies about a third of the cohort whatever C does.

Three things this raises

1. Does the right-hand panel need real units? It is drawn in squared standardised deviations, which no lab reports. The alternative is to label it “how far outside the range, squared” and drop the numbers.

2. Is C still worth a control? It is what makes the corridor visible and it is one of the notebook's three parameters — but with the kernel as the subject it is a second idea, and “simplify” may mean fixing it.

3. RBF is not drawable and is not offered. Its feature space is infinite-dimensional, so there is no right-hand panel for it. Offering rbf here would mean a control whose whole point — seeing the lifted space — goes blank for one of its options. The squared map is a genuine polynomial-family map and it is the one that can be shown.
