1 · Forward: one value of N(0, 1) per pixel

The answer. ε ~ N(0, I) is one draw of 784 numbers, one per pixel, each from N(0, 1) independently; xt is computed pixel by pixel, xt[i] = √āt x0[i] + √(1−āt) ε[i]. Nothing in the draft shows a single value, and the grey picture hides them: an image is drawn from −1 (black) to 1 (white), so the 32% of ε's values beyond ±1 all show as pure black or white. Hover any pixel of the three images: the same pixel is ringed in all three, and the card does the sum.

2 · Conditional: Forward and Training too?

Forward: no. The forward process never sees the label: xt = √āt x0 + √(1−āt) ε is the same whatever digit x0 is, so a conditional Forward page would be the Forward page again. Training: there is something to show. The loop is the unconditional one with c concatenated, but the eleven checkpoints of the conditional run show the shapes learned before the label: one sample a label from one shared starting noise, read by a classifier, is the same digit for every label until 100 batches (all 1s), mixed through epoch 3 (5 of 10 match their label), and 9 of 10 from epoch 4.
