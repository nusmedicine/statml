3 · Integrated gradients: the path, and why the steps matter

The mechanism under the strip: the words' embeddings scaled from the baseline (α = 0) to the note (α = 1), the predicted class's logit along the way, and the steps as a sum under the curve's slope. A press takes the next step; the bars grow as the gradients add up. The completeness line prints the sum of the bars against f(x) − f(baseline).
