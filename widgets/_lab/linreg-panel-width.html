How wide the two panels go

The bars run the full width and the two squares below them stop well short of it, which at the wide frame leaves 206 px of the row empty. Three ways to answer that — and one way that is not available.

Both panels have to stay square. The plane's whole claim is that only the diamond has corners; at unequal scales the L1 ball is not drawn as a diamond and the L2 ball is not drawn as a circle, so stretching it to fill the width would contradict the sentence in the subtitle. The predictions panel is measured against predicted in the same units, so y = x is only at 45° while it is square too. Square means wider costs taller, and that is the whole trade below.

Narrowing the equation and the bars to match the panels instead was the other suggestion. It is drawn as P4: it removes the ragged edge by wasting more of the row, not less.

What each one does to the widget's height

The rail holds the matrix and is 730.5 px tall, measured off the built widget and the same at every frame. The taller of the two columns is what the reader scrolls past, so a stage that grows into the rail's overhang is free until it passes it.
