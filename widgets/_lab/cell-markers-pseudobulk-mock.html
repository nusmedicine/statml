cell-markers · Tumour vs liver: one gene, over cells and over samples

The draft explains the difference in five lines of prose beside the map and shows it only as two volcanos. Below, one gene seen both ways, on one cluster's cells (Kupffer cells, about 24 per sample, patient effect 0.3). It opens on the unchanged gene with the smallest p over cells. Raise the sample effect: each sample's cells move up or down together, the Wilcoxon test over ~95 cells calls the gene, and the sum shows the four values behind them. Both p values are computed here, the Wilcoxon one as FindMarkers and the other by the DESeq2 engine of widget 78 on the four summed samples.

A · The cells collapse into their sample (one panel, animated)

"Sum each sample" slides each sample's cells to its column's centre and merges them into one point at the sample's pseudobulk value, on the same axis. The p line under the panel changes from the test over cells to the test over samples. Each cell moves only inside its own sample's column.

B · Cells and samples side by side (two panels, static)

The same gene, cells on the left and the four sums on the right, both p values always on screen. No press; the comparison is read directly.
