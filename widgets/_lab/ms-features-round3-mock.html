Slot 93 · ms-features · round 3: the widget read as a student

I walked the three pages in order as a student would, asking at each one what they can see, what they are told, and whether it connects to the page before. Ten findings, most serious first. Each comes with the smallest fix that answers it. The Identification change is the one that alters a layout, so it is drawn below at the widget's width, from the widget's own engine.

The widget's own point is that the m/z cannot tell these two apart, and a real untargeted run has no names at this stage.No names before Identification. 2 · Identification with every feature on the RT row (fixes 1, 4, 10)

After Match RT, row 3 and the table, at each RT window. The Feature control is gone. Each feature is a dot at its RT, labelled by its RT. A dot inside a standard's ±0.1 min band takes that standard's name, and a dot inside neither stays "glutaric or ethylmalonic".

All ten recommended. Fixes 1 and 2 matter most: one gives the last page something to show that the earlier pages connect to, the other stops page 1 from giving away the last page's answer. The rest are wording.
