Widget 35 candidate · metrics — both acts, mocked from the measured numbers

Settled 2026-08-30: one widget, two tabs (Numeric · Categorical, the notebook's own split), no ROC anywhere (widget 34 owns the threshold story — this one never draws a curve and never moves a cutoff by hand), simulated data, healthcare-framed. Every number on this page comes from the same seeded generators as _lab/metrics-measure.mjs, drawn live. The two misconceptions: numeric — that RMSE, MAE and R² are interchangeable summaries (one outlier moves RMSE +38% and MAE +16%, and R² is not an error but a comparison against predicting the mean); categorical — that accuracy says how good a classifier is (at separation 1.5, dropping prevalence 0.5 → 0.05 sends accuracy 0.77 → 0.95 while recall collapses 0.77 → 0.14, and "say nobody has it" nearly ties). Scenarios: numeric predicts body fat % (04-2's own example, values 5–40%); categorical is a simulated prognosis cohort, n = 200, whose model is what a default logistic regression trained at that prevalence does at the 0.5 cutoff.

§1 · Numeric — the stage is predicted vs actual, and each metric is an overlay on the same residuals

A is the plain stage: the notebook's scatter with the 45° perfect-prediction line (--c-reference) and one residual segment per patient. B is the teaching device: pick a metric and its anatomy appears on the same points — MSE draws each error as a literal square (area = squared error, so the one outlier's square dominates the sum), MAE draws the same errors as bars (lengths, all weighted alike). Shown with one outlier ON so the split is visible on the tiles. C is R²'s anatomy: the comparison model "predict the mean for everyone" appears on its horizontal line with its own (large) squares — R² = 1 − model squares / mean-model squares, proposed as an ease (points slide to the mean line and back).

left: the model's squares · right: the mean model's squares; R² = 1 − left/right

§2 · Categorical — the confusion matrix is the stage, and each metric lights its cells

D is the matrix two ways, same cohort (n 200, prevalence 0.3, separation 1.5, seed 1): left the roc-auc convention (counts, rows washed by their share — TN and TP darken as specificity and sensitivity rise), right each patient as a dot in their cell, coloured by true class — the count made tangible. E is the teaching device: picking a metric tile lights the cells it is made of — numerator solid, denominator outlined. Four metrics, four different readings of the same four numbers. F is the trap on the prevalence dial: the same separation at prevalence 0.3 and 0.1 — accuracy RISES as the disease gets rarer while recall halves and the all-negative baseline nearly ties.

§4 · Open picks

2) Matrix cells: counts + row wash (widget-34 sibling) or one-dot-per-patient — or wash with a small dot-grid inside?
3) The trap: does F live on the prevalence dial alone (one matrix, tiles moving, an all-negative baseline tile as the tell) — or as an explicit two-cohort comparison view?
