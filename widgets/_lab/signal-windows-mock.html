1 · The stage: eight subjects, each a recording in their own morphology, with BOTH annotations of your figure

Every subject has a rate, an amplitude, a QRS width, a T wave and a wander of their own. Two annotations sit on each recording: a recording annotation (the rhythm: regular or irregular, one label for the whole recording — what a PhysioNet fragment's class is, since it comes from the record) and event annotations (each ectopic beat, an interval of 80 samples). Which one becomes the window's label is the figure's two arms.

3 · Page 2, Split (cell 5): the windows dealt by window or by subject, and what a nearest neighbour scores

All eight subjects' windows as a deck, one row a subject, coloured by label; the held-out ones marked. The scorer is the simplest model there is: each held-out window takes the label of the most similar training window (correlation at the best shift), so the mechanism can be drawn — the held-out window over its match, and whose it is.
