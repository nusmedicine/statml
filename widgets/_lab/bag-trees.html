Showing the collection of trees

A mock-up, not a change to the bagging page. Right now that page tells you there are fifty trees and shows you one. These three put the collection on screen, so “166 distinct structures in 2000 resamples” becomes something you look at rather than something you are told. Same twelve samples, same bootstrap seed as the real page, so these are the actual trees it draws.

M1 · the grid replaces the tree panel

The collection takes the middle panel entirely. Nothing is added to the row, but the current tree is no longer readable at full size — you lose the ability to read its first split and compare it with page one’s.

M2 · the current tree, with the collection beneath it

The middle panel keeps the tree at readable size and gains a strip of miniatures under it. Both ideas survive, at the cost of a shorter tree and much smaller miniatures.

M3 · a shelf under the whole row

The three existing panels are untouched and the collection gets the full page width beneath them. The most legible miniatures of the three, and the only one that costs the current design nothing — but it needs vertical space, and the shelf is below the fold on a short window.
