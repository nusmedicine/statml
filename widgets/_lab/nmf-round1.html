§2 · The vocabulary — and your own course already has the word

07 - Cancer Mutation Analysis / 01-4 - Cohort - Mutational Signatures runs this exact algorithm on this exact matrix shape. Its cell 19 writes the model as M ≈ S · W — "M is the original trinucleotide matrix, S is the matrix of signatures, and W is the matrix of exposures" — and its three steps are calculate the matrix, estimate the number of signatures, extract signatures. Cell 21 is library(NMF). The matrix is even called tnm$nmf_matrix.

So the word you were reaching for is in your own notebook, and this widget can host two lessons the way widgets 20, 21 and 22 each host two. Two things follow. One: that notebook's step 2 chooses the rank by how stable the decomposition is across repeated runs (cophenetic correlation), which is the same quantity as the draft's agreement between starts — measured in §3 below, and it elbows in the right place. Two: the two notebooks disagree on letters — 05/04 calls the basis W, 07/01-4 calls the exposures W. The widget should pick one and probably name the objects rather than the letters.

§3 · PCA is a matrix decomposition — and your instinct about the difference is right

PCA is V ≈ L × S exactly as NMF is V ≈ W × H: same three matrices, same shapes. Drawing only the loadings hid that. But the difference you named is the real one, and it is sharper than signed-versus-not: PCA does one eigendecomposition and hands back every component, so choosing 2 or 6 is a choice about how many to keep; NMF has to be told the number before it starts and refits everything when you change it.
