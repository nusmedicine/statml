A · the 30 most variable genes in the unit, truly changed genes marked

Rows are the 30 genes with the largest SD across the samples in that unit, ranked; columns the samples with the group as a band on top; the cell is the value on the unit's own scale. A tick in the reference hue beside a row: that gene truly changed. Under counts the most variable genes are the highest-count ones, whether they changed or not, and the picture across the columns is depth and noise; under the vst they are the changed genes, and each row splits A | B. The tile reads truly changed among the 30. A unit change slides the rows that stay and fades the rest.

B · the notebook's own figure: the 30 top-called genes, the same rows under every unit

Rows fixed: the genes the test called, by padj, as the notebook's top-DEG heatmap. Under counts the scale is set by the largest count, so most rows are dark and the pattern is invisible; under log2(x + 1) and the vst every row reads and the A | B split is in each. C · the fold change shrunk step by step, the Shrinkage page's three curves over β

The likelihood of β is a normal at the estimate with the SE as its width; the prior fitted to every gene's estimate is a spike at zero (π0) and a normal of width τ, since a single normal erased the true effects with the false when it was measured; the posterior is their product, and its mean is the shrunk β. Left: the changed gene the page fits now, at a fair count, where the likelihood is sharp and the pull is slight. Right: an unchanged low-count gene, where the likelihood is wide and the posterior is mostly the spike. The Fit page would take four presses: Test · Fit the prior · Multiply by the prior · Shrink every gene, with the curves as a third panel in the top row and the gene walked chosen by pointing at the MA plot, as on Shrinkage.
