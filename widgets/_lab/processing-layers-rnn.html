processing-layers · Recurrent — three ways to draw the unrolled loop

The Recurrent page draws the values: four rows of 46 × 26 cells — Forward, Input, Reverse, Output — over five time steps, with arrows only between one hidden state and the next. The recurrence itself is not drawn. Nothing on the figure says that x_t and h_{t−1} both feed h_t, that the same f runs at every step, or that the row is one cell unrolled over time. These three options add that, at the real 550 stage width, with the geometry the widget already uses (PAD 14, so 522 usable; cell 46 × 26, gutter 26, so 72 between time steps, 62 for the row labels).

Every number is the widget's own: model.js's recurrent() off makeRng(1), which is the state the page computes at its defaults — sample 1, bidirectional, output . Colour: input --c-group-a, hidden states and outputs --c-empirical, the element being computed and what it reads --c-highlight. Arrows are 2px with a 9px head in --ink-2; the page draws its existing recurrence arrows at 1.5px in --ink-3, and they are redrawn here at the heavier weight so a panel is judged at one arrow weight rather than two.

A · arrows on the value rows

The four rows exactly as built, with the missing edges added: x_t up into h_t→ and down into h_t← in the row gaps, and each hidden cell into its half of y_t — the forward pass into the top three cells, the reverse pass into the bottom three. The two output edges have to travel past two rows, so they are routed in the column gutters. Costs no height at all.

B · his figure as a strip above the values

A diagram band above the value rows in dl-layer-rnn-bi.png's vocabulary at small size: x¹…x⁵ circles, h¹…h⁵ rounded boxes in a Forward row and a Reverse row, arrows between the boxes and from each x into both, and the two end states joining into an Output box on the right. The columns sit on the value columns' own centres, so the diagram and the numbers under it are the same five columns. On unidirectional the diagram loses its Reverse row, as the value rows do.

C · rolled beside unrolled, then the values

dl-layer-rnn.png's whole statement: the rolled cell on the left — one f box with x_t in, h_t out and the loop arrow back to itself labelled h_{t−1}, with the shared weights named once — and B's unrolled chain on the right, then the value rows below. Play would walk the chain and pulse the rolled cell's loop arrow at each step.

One step mid-walk, in the recommended option

Step 3 of the forward pass. h³→ is the element being computed; x³ and h²→ are what it reads. The diagram and the value rows light the same three things, and the two arrows into h³→ light with them — which is the edge the value rows alone cannot show.
