integration · Harmony in place of the anchors

Widget 80's stage — four samples, six cell types, tumour cells in the tumour samples only — with the patient shift at 0.5 rather than 0.9, and six clusters (why, under The measurement). Harmony alternates two steps until the cells stop moving: Cluster — soft k-means in the embedding, with a diversity penalty θ that lowers a cell's membership of a cluster its own batch already fills beyond its share, so each cluster is pushed to hold both batches; and Correct — within each cluster, each batch's centre is moved onto the cluster's centre, and every cell moves by its clusters' corrections, weighted by its membership. Circles are the clusters, sized by the cells they hold, split by the batch (left) and by the cell types (right) inside them. The arrows run from each batch's centre to the cluster's centre: the correction.

The measurement

_lab/integration-harmony-measure.mjs, Harmony as published (soft k-means with the diversity penalty, updated in blocks; a per-cluster ridge fit of the original embedding on batch), in two dimensions without the cosine normalisation. On the shipped stage (patient shift 0.9) Harmony fails for a reason that is not the lesson: a shift nearly as large as the distance between neighbouring types lays one patient's hepatocytes over the other's tumour cells, the soft clusters form across the two, and on five seeds of eight tumour cells end up among hepatocytes (19–28% of their neighbours) with Patient as the batch — where the anchors merge nothing. At 0.5 with six clusters (the package would use 20 for 600 cells; twelve drew in overlapping pairs), the clusters form within types: under Patient, θ = 2 mixes the patients (0.91–1.03, 1 is fully mixed) with at most 2% of tumour neighbours hepatocytes, on all eight seeds; under Tissue the tumour cells are pulled onto hepatocytes as θ grows, on every seed — 0.00–0.27 at θ = 0, 0.01–0.37 at θ = 2, 0.07–0.46 at θ = 4. The seeds differ widely: seed 1 reads 0% · 6% · 17%, seed 5 3% · 10% · 30%, and seeds 2 and 3 merge 20–27% with no penalty at all, because a soft cluster there spans the two types. The anchors under Tissue: 10–27%. They do not change with the shift (centring removes it at any size).
