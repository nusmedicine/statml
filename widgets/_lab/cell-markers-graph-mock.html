A · A "Graph" page before Clusters: 45 cells, one step a press

30 hepatocytes and 15 tumour cells — two types that share ten markers, so the kNN graph has bridges between them — laid out by their kNN graph (linked cells pulled together, all cells pushed apart); the neighbours are found in 20 principal components, as the widget does. k = 15 rather than Seurat's 20 for a graph of 45 cells; the prune stays at 1/15. Measured before choosing: three distinct types (Kupffer, immune, endothelial) gave no link between types at k = 10, nothing for the prune to remove and the same three communities at every resolution. The resolution re-runs Louvain on the same graph; it opens at 1.2, where the first pass leaves three communities and the aggregated pass merges two of them, as in the lesson's figure (below 1.2 the first pass already finds the two types; at 2 the hepatocytes break into about a dozen).
