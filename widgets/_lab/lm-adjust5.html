Widget 28 · round 8 — Fit and Adjust: one page or two?

The question Kenneth asked: the Fit tab's band families already show "the other held constant" — so what is Adjust for? The measured fact that decides where each idea CAN live: putting age in the model moves the BMI slope 1.72 → 1.50, which is 1.8–3.1 px at panel scale (measured in planning). The coefficient move is INVISIBLE in data space — no scatter can show it. So the pieces sort by space, not by tab: data space (the panels) can show the model's conditional reading and its fit; coefficient space (forest, table) is the only place the move is visible; the FWL view is the why. The band family IS the adjusted slope drawn — which is why any seam between "fit" and "adjust" leaks: they are one act seen in two spaces.

The seam has now failed twice. Round 5 moved the table off Fit to stop the overlap, and had to invent fit-quality bars to fill the hole (they duplicate the R² and spread tiles). Round 7 put band families on Fit and recreated the overlap from the other side. And the requested fix — Adjust keeps both marginal panels — makes Adjust a superset of Fit, leaving Fit nothing of its own. Two rounds of patching plus a vestigial tab is the evidence the seam is misplaced, not mis-drawn.

§3 · Candidate S — keep two tabs, seam redrawn: "one model" vs "models compared"

The honest two-tab seam is not fit/adjust but data space vs coefficient space: tab 1 = panels + quality (what THIS model says), tab 2 = forest + table + FWL (what happens ACROSS models). Steelman: it stages a lecture (walk the tabs in order), keeps each screen sparse, and the Adjust note choreographs a deliberate act — "toggle age and watch the coefficient move". Against it: the pills sit on both tabs, so on tab 1 the punchline of a click still plays off-screen; the band family still whispers "held constant" on tab 1, so the seam still leaks; and tab 1's only exclusive content duplicates the tiles. If S is picked, the fix is names and captions, not structure: tab 1 stops saying "fit" (say The model), tab 2 stops saying "adjust" (say Compare models), and the band family's caption names the slope it draws ("slope 1.50 at every age — this model's reading").
