tensors — draw extents, stage geometry and the region map

Three measurements at the two stage widths the collection cares about, 550 (the fingerprint canvas, in a 900px frame) and 770 (a full desktop, in a 1120px frame). Extents wraps fillText and reports any string whose box leaves the canvas, plus any NaN. Geometry wraps fillRect and strokeRect and reports the four region rectangles and the cell size the fit chose. Targets is the one that no pixel hash can take: for every index label the Basics drawing writes, it dispatches a pointer move at the centre of that label and asks the widget whether it is over a region — so a target that has drifted off its own label reads as a miss here and nowhere else.
