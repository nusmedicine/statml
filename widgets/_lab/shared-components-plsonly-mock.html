2 · Factorize, then the Shortcut

Factorize keeps Score · Rebuild · Subtract (X ≈ t cᵀ + E, unchanged, PLS only). A fourth press, Shortcut, answers "how is this different from SVD": the loop was a slow way to compute one SVD. Left: the covariance of every mRNA feature with every methylation feature, X₁ᵀX₂ / n. Its first singular pair is exactly the two arrows the loop converged to. Right, for contrast: the SVD of one block, X₁ alone, gives the dashed largest-variance direction.

Recommend Shortcut as Factorize's fourth press, after Subtract: by then the student has the arrows (Concept) and the matrices (Factorize), and the Shortcut names the computation behind both, with the one sentence that separates the methods: PCA is the SVD of X; PLS is the SVD of X₁ᵀX₂. The formula card at that press reads w₁, w₂ = first singular pair of X₁ᵀX₂.
