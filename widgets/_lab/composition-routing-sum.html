composition · Routing, what the weighted sum looks like

Kenneth on review of the composition draft: "is there supposed to be a depiction of weighted sums?" The Routing page draws an outlined box spanning the three branch columns, labelled weighted sum, with nothing inside it. The operation the page is named for is the one thing the page does not show. Four ways to fill it, each drawn at the widget's own geometry at 550 and 770, in mode: soft at sample 0, and each of the two candidates in play drawn again at mode: hard. C is what is built today. D is the later question: Kenneth asked whether the box should hold the full [4, 20] per branch, as the Gating and Branching pages do, so a student is not left asking why a [4, 20] branch shows one row.

Every number is the widget's. ROUTE_WEIGHTS, ROUTE_TOP and routing(mode) are imported from ../composition/model.js, and so are PAD, COLGAP, BOX_H, EDGE_H, LINE, BAND_HEAD, CAP_GAP, CAPTION_H, TEXT_GAP, MIN_BOX, sizesAt, fitSizes, besideFits and routeMinDiag — so the fit pass on this page is the widget's fit pass, not a copy of it. The drawing is copied from composition-mock.html §2 and from main.js drawRoute; only the block inside the box is new. WBLOCK is 64 today, four WROW rows of gate weights, and that is the height every "grows by" below is measured against.

Colour: branch 1 --c-group-a, branch 2 --c-group-b, branch 3 --c-group-c, every result --c-empirical, the framed column and the chosen row --c-highlight, the gate column --ink-2. A strip cell is the branch's hue shaded by its value; in A, C and D that shading is multiplied by the weight, which is the attention precedent — a smaller weight is a paler row, and the row that contributes nothing is nearly blank.

§1 · A · the chosen sample's rows

Four strips of 20 cells inside the box, one row of the tensor each: the three products αᵢ(x) · fᵢ(x) for sample 0, a + down the left, and the column total in --c-empirical under a rule. The weight is printed at each strip's right. No digits go in the strips: 20 cells at two decimals is 800px and the box is 406px at 550.

§2 · B · all four samples

The three branch outputs as full [4, 20] bands with the [4, 3] weights beside them, one weight cell per sample row, and the combined [4, 20] band under. The chosen sample's row is lit in every band. The bands carry fᵢ(x) itself rather than the product, so the multiply is the one step this candidate leaves the reader to do.

§3 · C · A plus one feature's arithmetic

A's strips, one column framed through all four of them, and that column's arithmetic printed under the box with the widget's own numbers. In the widget the framed column is the one under the pointer and the line is reserved at rest, which is the hover idiom the attention page already uses. The mock frames the column with the most non-zero terms: every branch ends in a ReLU, and at column 0 all three branches are zero, so a line printed there would carry the form and none of the arithmetic.

§5 · The fit question at 770

Routing puts the code beside the diagram at 770 and under it at 550, and the code column is 350px whatever the frame. A block wide enough for 20 cells is a new claim on the diagram's width, so the left panel forces the code beside at 770 and draws the strips at whatever cell size is then left. The right panel is the same figure with the code under, which is what the fit pass chooses.

§6 · C at mode: hard

One branch is taken and the other two contribute nothing. The two untaken strips are drawn as empty cells, which is the same block saying a different thing, and the arithmetic line loses its products.
