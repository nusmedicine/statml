cell-qc · where the doublet material sits

Two separate things are wrong on the Metrics page and they have different fixes. The order: the doublet band sits BETWEEN the four distributions and the two scatters, so the material about the first three numbers is split in half by the material about the fourth. The readout: four tiles squeezed into 114px columns with half the width empty — a core bug, diagnosed below. Every diagram here is to scale, at the two widths that matter: 746px, the lesson's own frame, and 534px, the harness's.

1 · The readout, and why it is squished

.w-readout is repeat(auto-fit, minmax(104px, 1fr)). Auto-fit collapses the tracks nothing sits in, and the surviving tracks then take the whole width. A row break defeats it: .w-stat-break is grid-column: 1 / -1, so every track has something in it and none of them collapses. Measured on the live widget: six tracks of 114.4px with the break, five of 139.7px with its span removed, four of 177.6px with it hidden altogether.

As it is — six tracks of 114px

The break as a column-start instead — two tracks of 367px

The fix is in core: renderReadout marks the tile AFTER a break instead of inserting a spanning div, and the CSS sets grid-column-start: 1 on it. Nothing spans, so the empty tracks collapse. It reaches four other widgets that use a break — enrichment, gwas, metrics, naive-bayes — and widens their tiles the same way. No canvas moves, so no fingerprint state changes.
