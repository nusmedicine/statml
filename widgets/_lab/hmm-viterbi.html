Same toy, same seed, same trellis (2 patterns × N days). The algorithm has two passes: forward, one column at a time — into each node come two candidate paths, one per previous state, and only the better one survives; then trace back from the best final node along the survivors. Drag step through both passes. Node fill is the column's relative score (the best-path probability ending there, normalised within the column — the raw product is below 0.01 within a dozen days, which is why every textbook worked example uses logs or per-column scaling).
