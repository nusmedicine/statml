94's third page · a graph that is not a molecule · mock

His ask: "mock another page for a non-molecular example … want to inspire students that graphs are versatile … patients? omics?" 09-1 cell 0 lists four domains — clinical (an EHR graph), biomedical knowledge (drug–protein–disease), systems biology (PPI, gene regulation) and molecular. The Graph page is abstract and the Molecule page is the molecular one, so the third page should be a domain where the nodes, the edges and what is predicted all differ from a molecule. A · EHR: the tables of PHM5005 02-1 as a graph

Your tidy_patient, tidy_visit and tidy_diagnosis (4 patients, 9 visits, 16 diagnoses), joined on VisitID and PatientID: one node per patient and per diagnosis, one edge per patient–diagnosis pair. Patients 2 and 4 are linked only through URTI. Node features differ by type (age and sex for a patient; none for a diagnosis but its one-hot).

B · Patients by their omics profile: the edges are built

Twelve tumours measured on two genes (log2 expression of ESR1 and ERBB2; simulated, two subtypes), drawn where their measurements put them, so the drawing is the feature space. Each patient is joined to its k nearest; the edge weight is the similarity. Some subtypes are known, the rest are unknown — the node-level task of 09-1 cell 24's table (predict disease subtype for each patient), and the setting GCN was introduced for. Left k = 2, right k = 4: the edges joining two subtypes grow with k. Patient similarity networks are an established method; fusing one per omics type is similarity network fusion (Wang et al., Nature Methods 2014), which reaches PHM5003 10's multi-omics.

C · A knowledge graph: drugs, proteins, diseases

Three node types and two relations (a drug targets a protein; a protein is associated with a disease), the relation one-hot in edge_attr. The dashed edge is the kind of link a model is asked for: a drug and a disease with no edge yet. Illustrative, not curated.

Recommended: B, Patients. It is the one candidate that teaches something the other two pages cannot: in a molecule the edges are given, here they are chosen, and the same patients make a different graph at a different k. It also carries the other task level — a label per node, some unknown — and it is the bridge to PHM5003 10 (subtypes from omics). A is the warmest callback but tiny (four patients) and its lesson is a join; C is the most "inspiring" picture and the least checkable. A Patients page would sit between Graph and Molecule, with k 1–4 and the same hover; still no training (that is 95).
