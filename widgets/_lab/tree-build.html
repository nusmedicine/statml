Tree-based methods

One widget, three pages. Twelve samples and two features on the first page — small enough that every candidate split can be scored on screen, and the arithmetic checked by hand. Protocol: candidate thresholds are midpoints of consecutive distinct values, the score is the weighted Gini of the two children, ties go to the lower feature index then the lower threshold. Verified against scikit-learn: same three splits, same thresholds, same leaves.
