§1 · The U as a U

The encoder's blocks step in toward the centre as they go down, the decoder's step out as they come up, and the bottleneck is at the bottom between them — your figure's staircase. The slabs keep the draft's rule (height follows H × W, width follows C) and the skips run across the U, shortest at the bottom. Drawn at 550 and 770, depth 4 and depth 2. The step between levels grows with the width, so the U opens at 770.

§2 · A click on a block: the operation, with real numbers

The clicked block is framed on the U and a band under it depicts what that block does to one position, on the maps the forward pass computed: the block's input maps (four channels drawn, the count printed), the operation on one unit, and the output maps. Five kinds of block, five depictions: DoubleConv (a 3 × 3 window from every input channel, one kernel slice each, the sum and bias, then BatchNorm and ReLU; the second conv is named, not drawn), MaxPool (a 2 × 2 window, the largest kept), ConvTranspose (one input cell scattered to a 2 × 2 patch through the 2 × 2 kernel), concat (the encoder's channels and the upsampled channels side by side, the count doubling, the size unchanged) and the head (one pixel's channels through a 1 × 1 conv to a logit, the sigmoid, the threshold). The input here is 16 × 16 and base 4, so the numbers are drawable; the shapes printed on the U stay those of the chosen Input and Base channels.
