The call

The VAE page's scaleShared ±4 · Each its own · Autoscale · Zoom (the four above). On the Autoencoder page the map stays on its own scale (±25); on Conditional both maps are VAE models and share ±4 in every option.
Recommend Autoscale: both clouds stay readable at every checkpoint, the moving axes make the spreading itself visible (the left view zooming out to about ±30 while the right comes back near ±3.5), and it needs no control. Zoom is the most flexible but adds a control that is set once and forgotten.
