§0 · The title and the subtitle

The lesson's title is Mendelian Randomization Study. Two subtitles, each stating the concept in the register of the shipped ones.

§1 · Step 1 — one SNP as a trial

The causal-structures composition: the graph on the left, the cohort on the right. A the finished step: 2,000 people, the observational fit through every one of them, the three genotype groups' centroids and the line through them — the single-SNP ratio — with the true effect revealed. B the same with Colour by the confounders on: the tint runs along the observational slope and is the same in all three genotype groups, which is the randomisation. C mid-run, after the second beat: the people and the observational fit, before the centroids. The run is four beats — the people, the observational fit, the genotype groups, the ratio — and Step's label names the next one.

§2 · The graph, and the three arrows it must not have

The DAG panel alone, at the width it has on steps 1 and 3. The fork's two arrows are red because the path through the confounders is open and stays open — MR does not close it, it routes around it. The two forbidden arrows are drawn as ghosts, click targets a press turns on. A every assumption holding; B the exclusion restriction broken (a share of SNPs with a direct path); C independence broken (the confounders reach the SNPs); D relevance weak (the SNPs-to-BMI arrow thin); E no confounding at all — the fork's arrows are absent and the observational estimate is right. The verdict line under each names the mechanism, as widget 26's does.

§3 · Step 2 — the two GWAS

Two strips and the scatter forming under them. The exposure GWAS on top — each SNP's effect on BMI with its interval, in the order the run takes them (strongest first) — the outcome GWAS below it on the same columns, and the scatter of one against the other filling in as SNPs arrive. A mid-run at 30 of 79, harmonised. B the finished step with Harmonise OFF: the outcome GWAS reports half the SNPs on its other allele, those effects wear an open marker and sit mirrored on the scatter, and the slope is gone. C the finished step harmonised.

§4 · Step 3 — the estimate

The lesson's scatter with the graph beside it, so the violations act where they are drawn. A Estimator IVW, every assumption holding, the truth revealed: IVW full, Egger and the median faded, the observational slope dotted as the reference. B Estimator All. C exclusion restriction broken on 30% of SNPs, Estimator MR Egger: the pleiotropic SNPs marked, the intercept off zero, the slope holding. D relevance weak (exposure GWAS 8,000) in one sample, Estimator IVW: every interval wide and the estimate dragged toward the observational. E independence broken, Estimator All: all three off. The readout under each is the step's tiles.
