Slot 89 · the filter and the MNAR cut-off, on one scale

The confusion: two thresholds, described in two directions (“measured in at least half”, “missing from 80%”). Here both are written the same way, as the proportion of a group's samples in which the protein was measured, and drawn on your data: the 1,337 proteins of 01-4's results table, each placed by how many of its 11 cancer and 11 healthy samples had a value. Simulation numbers are the widget's engine over 20 seeds (_lab/imputation-filter-measure.mjs).

The two never need translating into “missing” percentages. “Missing from 30%” is just “measured in 70%” said backwards, and it belongs to the filter, not to impute.mixed. So 0.2 stays 0.2; there is no 0.3 anywhere.

3 · What each filter does to the test — simulation, 20 seeds, 600 proteins, 110 real differences

Read across a row. Any filter fixes kNN (FDR 26% → 4–5%): kNN's false calls are proteins seen in one or two samples, and every filter removes them. 50% and 70% behave almost the same. 100% keeps only 249 of 600 proteins and loses half the real differences (54 of 110 survive), and a third of the absent-in-healthy proteins with them. The filter barely changes the minimum or Mixed, which already handle near-empty proteins.

4 · The words, proposed

Notebook, the filter sentence in “In practice”

**Filter first.** Proteins measured in only a few samples carry little information to impute from, so they are
often removed first: a common choice keeps proteins measured in at least half (50%) of the samples of one group;
70% is also used, and the strictest keeps only proteins measured in every sample of one group. The threshold is a
convention, not a rule, and `tidyproteomics`' own workflow does not include this step.

Notebook, beside impute.mixed

`max_measured_prop` answers a different question from the filter. The filter decides which proteins are kept;
`max_measured_prop` decides which of the kept proteins count as MNAR: those measured in this proportion of the
samples of some group, or fewer (0.2 of 11 = 2 samples).

Widget, the filter control

Filter before imputing     None · 50% · 70% · 100%
  detail: keep proteins measured in at least this share of the samples of one group
