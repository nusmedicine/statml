Widget 27 + the residual plot — where it goes, what it can honestly show

The framing that fits THIS widget's lesson: the residual view shows the residuals of the reader's own line, live with the sliders. A wrong intercept shows as the band sitting OFF ZERO (at (70, 2): +9.9 mmHg); a wrong slope shows as a TILT in the trend (−0.28 mmHg per BMI unit); and the least-squares fit is the line whose residuals are a flat band at zero — which is literally where the walk stops. What this view CANNOT teach on the real data: curvature or funnel patterns meaning "the model class is wrong" — sysBP ~ BMI is an adequate fit, so that lesson stays with the synthetic-data diagnostics widget if it is wanted.

§1 · What the residual view shows — your line, then the fit (full width)

Residuals against fitted values, the notebook's own axes. The trend line through the residuals is what makes offset and tilt legible (autoplot draws a smoother for the same reason; this one is the linear trend, which is exact here because the model is a line). Zero ruled. The right panel is the honest fine print: at the FIT the trend is flat at zero by construction, and what remains is the real data's mild wedge — visible, unexplained, and deliberately uncaptioned.

§2 · Where it lives in the widget

A — a View toggle on the scatter panel (Data / Residuals), the panel transforming in place with the slide motion widgets 26/28 use; drawn at the real gate-open width (~250px): still legible, because the view is a band, not a cloud that needs width. Height unchanged. B — a permanent strip under the scatter (~80px): always visible, no toggle, live with the sliders — but it costs every state ~90px of height and repeats the wash's information while the line is held.

What A adds to the widget's own lesson: offset = b₀ wrong, tilt = b₁ wrong, flat at zero = fitted — the residual view as a second reading of the same search, and the stopping condition made visible. What it does not add: the wrong-model-class patterns (curve, funnel) that 05-01 §2's pictures are really about — the real data cannot produce them on demand. If both are wanted, A here plus the synthetic diagnostics widget later do not overlap: this one reads YOUR line's residuals, that one reads a MODEL's.
