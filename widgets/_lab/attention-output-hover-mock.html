Widget 83 · hover on the Output step · mock

The Output step as it is, four queries computed, the last aspirin; head 4. Live: pick a mode and hover. As on the other steps, hover reaches computed rows only, once the press is still, and moving off returns the press's own query.

  • O1 · a query: hover a computed row of the Features with attention column, or of the α matrix: that token becomes the query, its weights fill the Weights column, and the Sum shows its zᵢ.
  • O2 · a term: hover a key's row (its weight or its vⱼ): its term αᵢⱼ vⱼ is drawn under the sum, the values scaled by the weight, with the share it is of the whole.
  • O3 · one number: hover a single number of any vⱼ or of zᵢ: that column is outlined down every vⱼ and in zᵢ, and the sum for that one number is written out, Σⱼ αᵢⱼ vⱼ,d, term by term.

Round 2, his catch: with O2 and O3 the pointer has left the row that chose the query, so the query falls back to the press's. Two ways to choose the query and still take a number apart:

  • QA · click to pin: click a computed row of zⱼ or of α and that query stays; O3 then takes apart any number of it. Click the row again to let go. In the widget the pin is a parameter (a rail control Query: Latest · 1 … L, and in the link), since a click writes a parameter or nothing.
  • QB · hover a number of zⱼ: no pin. The zⱼ column is the outputs, so hovering number d of zⱼ explains that very number: query j into the Weights column and the Sum, number d outlined down every vⱼ, and the sum written out as an equation under the Sum. One target picks both.
Mode

Recommended (round 2): QB, with O1's α-matrix rows kept for reading back a query's weights. The number being explained is the thing under the pointer, so no second hover has to keep a first one alive, and nothing new goes in the rail or the link. QA works, but it adds a control whose value the press would fight over (a pinned query and the press's next query both want the Weights column).
