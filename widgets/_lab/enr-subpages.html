Five things this needs you to settle

1 · Does the gate survive the split?

Right now the score table blanks until the walk finishes, so it cannot spoil the walk. With All pathways as its own page the reader has chosen to go there, which is already an opt-in. Keep the gate (they must walk once, on page 3, before page 4 fills in), or drop it now that arriving is deliberate?

2 · Does removing the metric from ORA make ORA independent of it?

Hiding the control does not change what it does: ORA's list is still the top 60 of whatever ranking the metric picked, so a reader who sets Signed significance on page 3 and returns to page 1 gets different ORA numbers with no visible cause. The clean fix is to pin ORA to fold change — which also matches cell 9 — and let the metric belong to the score alone. Or leave it silent.

3 · Where does the view control go?

Drawn here as a second segmented group under Method. The alternative is one four-way control, or putting the view switch on the stage as tabs above the figure rather than in the rail.

4 · What does the readout say on a table page?

Drawn as collection-level numbers rather than the selected pathway's. The alternative is to keep it identical across both views so nothing moves under the reader.

5 · Does the widget get taller or shorter?

Splitting halves the vertical load: page 3 loses the table, page 4 loses the profile, walk and histogram. That is the main win beyond clarity — the score tab is currently ~610px and would drop to roughly 420 and 240.
