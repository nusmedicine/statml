Would the other widgets benefit from the side layout?

These are the real widgets, not schematics. Each is loaded in a 1200 px frame and the side layout is injected — the same DOM buildShell makes under layout:"side", and the same CSS already in tokens.css. Core carries a ResizeObserver, so every canvas genuinely repaints at its new width. Heights are measured after that repaint.

The bar to clear is 900 px — a laptop viewport, and roughly what a projector shows without scrolling. A widget only benefits if it is over that now and the change brings it under, or close.
