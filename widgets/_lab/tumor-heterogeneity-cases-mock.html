1 · Your questions

questionanswer
What are the solid and dashed lines?The two inherited copies, one from each parent. Solid is the copy the mutation arose on; dashed is the other parent's copy. 2 + 0 reads “two copies of one parent's chromosome, none of the other's”.
In 2 + 0, must both copies carry the mutation?Only if the mutation happened before the copy was duplicated (VAF 1.0 at purity 1). If it happened after, one copy carries it (VAF 0.5), which is what the Mutated copies control was for. Both are real cells.
Does the VAF need the parental copies?No. The VAF depends on two numbers only: the copies in a tumor cell and how many of them carry the mutation. Which parent a copy came from never enters it. So page 1 can drop the solid/dashed distinction and the major + minor notation. Page 3 needs the parental split in one place only: an allele-specific call says how many copies could carry the mutation, which is cell 24's ASCAT (major/minor) and is introduced there.
