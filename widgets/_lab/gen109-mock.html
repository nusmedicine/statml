Page 1 — Sampling: one distribution, three ways to draw from it

84 generates greedily throughout. This page is where greedy, temperature and top-k are the same distribution read three ways. The honest finding: a mid-clause prompt is deterministic, so generation must start at the note's opening, which is the only place with real uncertainty.

why generation starts at the opening, not at 84's prompt

The next-token distribution at the note's opening (the [BOS] position)

The same distribution under each rule, and what 200 draws produce

reading Greedy gives one note every time. Temperature reshapes the whole distribution (a slider in the widget); higher T lets the rare openings through. Top-k keeps only the k most probable tokens, then renormalises — at small k the tail openings vanish, at k≥10 it is indistinguishable from plain sampling because only tokens are live here.

Entropy falls to zero once the clause is chosen

the teaching hook The opening word is a real choice (high entropy); after one or two tokens the grammar locks the rest, so the decoding rule only ever changes the beginning. This figure is why.

Page 2 — Conditioning: a token at the front steers every later token

A sibling model trained with a condition token at the front of each note (two readable conditions from the grammar — a treatment clause [TREAT] or a negated finding [NEG]), the prefix dropped 20% of the time so an unconditional fallback exists. The only thing that makes the token a condition is its position and that training always put it there — a different mechanism from 84's cross-attention.

The first-position distribution under each prefix

measured The two conditioned distributions are disjoint (total-variation distance of 1). Dropping the prefix gives a clean blend of the two — that is the “remove the prefix” toggle: the fallback sits visibly between the conditioned ones.

What to pick

Q1 — Sampling: which decoding arms? A. Greedy · Temperature (slider) · Top-k (small k, 3–5) — all three, recommended (top-k's tail-clipping is visible only at small k).
B. Greedy · Temperature only (cut top-k, since at k≥10 it equals plain sampling on a ~9-token live set).
C. All three, with top-k's k as a slider so the reader sees it collapse into plain sampling as k grows.
Q2 — Sampling: open with the entropy-by-position strip? A. Yes — lead with it, it explains why only the opening changes, recommended.
B. No — keep it as a hover/footnote under the distribution.
Q3 — Conditioning: which two conditions? A. [TREAT] treatment clause · [NEG] negated finding — disjoint, clearly different sentences, recommended.
B. Discharge sense: [HOME] (planned discharge home) · [WOUND] (discharge from wound) — the same word discharge under two senses (ties to 84's discharge example).
C. Outcome: [RECOVERED] · [DETERIORATED].
Q4 — Conditioning: show the dropped-prefix fallback? A. Yes, as a toggle that removes the condition token and shows the blended distribution, recommended (it proves conditional and unconditional are one model).
B. No — two prefixes side by side is enough.
Q5 — one widget or two pages? A. One widget, two pages Sampling · Conditioning, recommended (both are “controlling generation”; the card can hold both objectives).
B. Two separate widgets.
