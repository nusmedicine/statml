Variant B is codified: step and play are fenced into one cluster, Reset sits outside it, and all seven widgets now fit one line — including bootstrap and permutation-test, whose fourth lead button simply sits before the cluster. No special arrangement is needed for four buttons, so the earlier “E2” is withdrawn.

1 · What is actually wrong, as shipped

Four problems, and only two are length. Widths are measured at the real drive font (--fs-md), not counted in characters.

2 · What a good label has to do here

The existing rule in core is “labels are the widget's to name: a Galton board drops balls, it does not draw samples. Generic verbs make a widget feel like a demo of a framework.” That is about specificity and it is right, but it is not sufficient — it permits both of the collisions above. Four requirements, in priority order:

  1. Name the widget's own noun. Balls, samples, studies, shuffles, genes — never “item” or “step”. This is the existing rule.
  2. A lead must not read like a step. The lead happens once and greys out for good; the step repeats forever. If the two are near-synonyms, the greying-out looks like a fault instead of a lesson.
  3. The same class of action reads the same across the arc. A student meets these six weeks apart. If one widget's once-only button and another's repeatable button share a phrasing, the transfer is actively harmful.
  4. Fit the narrowest track it will ever appear in. 262 px rail, so a step label has roughly 130 px once Play and Reset are paid for.
