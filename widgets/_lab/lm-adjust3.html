Widget 28 · round 4 — the two diagnoses, drawn

§1 · Fit and Adjust overlap because the TABLE is on the wrong tab

The three-model table is a COMPARISON — it is Table-2-fallacy material, and parking it on Fit spoils Adjust's punchline before the reader gets there. The redistribution: Fit keeps what fitting owns (the plane family, the equation, R² and residual spread — the 1D → 2D extension of one model) and Adjust collects every comparison: the table, the forest, and the FWL slide. And the forest stops whispering — the move is ANNOTATED, so a reader who missed the ease still gets the sentence.

§2 · Collinearity — three ways to make the failure visible

What actually fails is ATTRIBUTION, not prediction — R² and the residuals do not move when the twin enters; the model just can no longer say WHICH twin did it. Three additions, cheapest first: A the DAG grows the twin node with its measured correlation on the dashed link — the problem as a picture, in the panel the reader already watches; B a small twin-against-BMI scatter — "the model cannot tell these apart", the correlation graph itself; C the split WANDERS — the same forest at three seeds: the twins trade one effect back and forth (their SUM barely moves — bracketed), while R² stays identical. Reseed without a twin and nothing anywhere changes; reseed with one and the attribution scrambles. That contrast is the failure.
